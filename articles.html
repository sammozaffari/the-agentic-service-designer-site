Work

Eight projects, one employer, 2024 to 2026

All of this is employment work at KFC South Pacific, written up with the organisation, vendors, sites and people removed. The first four are the cases I would show first. The second four cover employee experience, service quality, the self-service channel and the drive-thru.

Lead case · Safety incident reporting, 2025

A reporting system built for one workflow and used by another

The business assumed location explained why some restaurants reported more incidents than others. I chose the sample to test that. Location did not predict it. The manager did, and the recommendations changed to match.

Research
13 in-depth interviews, 55 survey responses, 2 walkthroughs with the safety team, 8 restaurants
Output
A five-stage service blueprint, 232 linked insight records, a findings report, three proposals with prototypes, and the tool that holds it all
Status
Delivered December 2025: findings report, blueprint, roadmap, form prototypes. Implementation sits with the platform, safety and training teams.
