Use cases

The article series closes with seven engagement studies. Each is a fictional or composite Service Design scenario paired with a declared evidence class. Read the project outcomes, people, metrics and conversations as illustrative unless the linked receipt says otherwise. Platform facts are current only where backed by the shared release evidence: 29 tools, 225 MCP resources, 10 prompts, the ten-role contract, and the bounded CLI/MCP/BUZZ/DSH surfaces. This page is a catalogue of workflows, not a claim that every article is a live host run.

Historical run records are intentionally retained for provenance: the article modernization evidence, prior BUZZ disposable receipt, and prior DSH integration receipt describe earlier runs. Any 224-resource value inside those records is historical; it is not the current 232-resource release inventory.

Aurora Vento — servitization on the plugin

Article 32 · industrial manufacturing · 22 weeks · read the study

A hydraulic access vendor with 41,000 machines in the field, earning 31% of service revenue from aftersales add-ons, re-architected around outcome contracts. The same plugin surface on a B2B servitization problem: the Researcher builds the register, the Mapper frames the opportunity, the Measurer wires internal service quality to KPI rows with baselines — never sentiment — and the Critic gates every review-gated artifact. Service revenue share moved 31%→48%, renewals 71%→88%, mean time to repair 5.2→2.9 days, first-time fix 61%→79%. The study is the clearest demonstration of where the platform's job ends and the analyst's begins: the engine holds evidence, typed artifacts, and gates; the contract strategy is the designer's.

Halo Care — the Critic on every gate, under clinical constraints

Article 33 · telehealth · 20 weeks · read the study

A telehealth provider scaled from 400k to 1.4M patients and broke at the seams: 22-minute virtual waits, 18% did-not-attend, no context handover between clinicians. The engagement rebuilt the care journey with the Critic gating every review-gated artifact — and it states the governance story plainly: the engine has no "governance config", so clinical-safety sign-off runs as Critic verdicts persisted as review-veto-log artifacts with severity-graded rows and mandatory fixes, plus gate minutes the human clinical lead signs. Virtual wait fell 22→6 minutes, DNA 18%→7%, first-visit resolution 61%→78%, clinician eNPS −10→+25.

Vela Bank — compliance as review rows, SME banking

Article 34 · fintech · 19 weeks · read the study

A digital SME bank winning on price and losing on friction: 9-day onboarding at 62% abandonment, 11-day loan decisions. The study applies the plugin surface to financial services, where the hard gate is compliance — carried as three real mechanisms, each stated plainly: Critic sd_review verdicts persisted as review-veto-log artifacts, risk-committee minutes registered as document-kind evidence, and human sign-off at each phase gate. Onboarding fell 9→2.1 days, abandonment 62%→31%, loan decisions 11→4.5 days, NPS 12→44, monthly churn 4.8%→2.1%. It is also the best worked example of the twelve machine-gated method skills keeping onboarding facts evidence-cited enough for a risk committee to audit.

Read next: the skills reference for the gated canon and the CLI for the commands quoted throughout.

Sable & Slate — the write-path Critic, in luxury hospitality

Article 35 · luxury boutique hotels · 17 weeks · read the study

A nine-property group digitized its way to impersonality — "the technology remembers nothing about me" — and rebuilt the stay around a guest-context memory that serves the cast, not the screen. The Critic is the star of this study: a veto requires at least one defect row with a concrete fix, the veto demotes the artifact to draft, and the whole exchange persists as a review-veto-log linked to its target. A privacy convention becomes review-row law — consent carried as review rows citing a consent-spec evidence row — with no custom machinery. Check-in fell 14→4 minutes, staff turnover 34%→21%, repeat stays 38%→52%, NPS 41→68.

Read next: the artifact kind reference for the veto-log anatomy and the agent reference for the Critic's method discipline.
