The author

Sam Mozaffari

Sam Mozaffari is an applied AI practitioner with a designer's taste. Seven years across enterprise research, product design and service design, the last three and a half deliberately integrating AI into all of it: software development, research, prototyping and UI. His working method is fully agentic: Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Gemini and a ten-role harness of his own design, with an adversarial critic on every gate. He designs service experiences for a global quick-service restaurant network and runs the platform this book documents: 80 methods encoded as SOPs, a 30-tool engine, and 50 published evidence-cited case studies — 7 client engagements and 43 practice studies.

This is his book, and it is a living document for agentic service designers. Every platform change re-enters it through the same evidence gates the book teaches: methods as code, receipts over claims, human phase gates. When the harness changes, the book changes with it.

What's inside

The full practice — theory, methods, and the platform that runs them

Part I grounds the operating system. Parts II–IV walk the discipline end to end — research ops, mental models, five-lane blueprinting, the sixth agentic lane, orchestration, ideation, prototyping, recovery, quality governance. Part V is new in this edition: four dedicated platform chapters on the ASD MCP server (29 tools, 232 resources, 10 prompts), the plugin host packs and Claude Code native plugin, BUZZ multi-agent engagements with Canvas indexing, and the DeepSeek Harness Studio workbench — each with real screenshots, exact commands and honest evidence boundaries. Part VI closes with SOPs-as-code, frontline enablement, Good Services audits, a complex fictional mastercase run across every surface, and the future of the practice.

Real captures

BUZZ channel runs, Canvas round trips, DSH Studio sessions and CLI gate reviews — screenshots from bounded runs, captioned with what they prove and what they don't.

Dedicated platform depth

MCP JSON-RPC walkthroughs, client configs for Claude Code / OpenCode / Codex, the twelve-target pack matrix, persona-pack identity audits.

Fictional mastercase

Meridian Bay Ferries: one engagement across CLI, plugin, MCP, BUZZ and Studio — evidence chain, a Critic veto with remediation, human phase gates throughout.

Built on the corpus

TISDD, Kumar, Stickdorn, Downe, SDL and the Service Profit Chain — cited through 80 methods encoded as SOPs and served back out as 228 MCP corpus resources.
