Agentic Service Design Fundamentals

The AI-First Service Design Operating System, 10-Agent Team Topology & Designer-Agent Loop

The foundation course for the whole curriculum. Replaces static sticky notes and workshop theater with an executable, AI-driven Service Design Operating System. Connects classical service theory (Vargo & Lusch's Service-Dominant Logic, Service Profit Chain) to modern agentic systems. Teaches the 10-role Eve-style agent team topology, the autonomy spectrum, and the sovereign human decision gate.

View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 01.1 The Death of Workshop Theater: Moving to an Executable Service OS (45 min) — Why sticky-note practice decays (TISDD Ch. 4/8, Kumar Ch. 1), and how a content-addressed evidence store plus fail-closed method contracts restore provenance and repeatability.
  • 01.2 Service-Dominant Logic & Value Co-Creation in the Age of AI (50 min) — Vargo & Lusch's five axioms as evidence discipline; co-creation vs. co-production; value co-destruction; `sd_evidence_add` quotes as FP10 value records; `service-profit-chain-mapping` as SDL-aware measurement.
  • 01.3 The 10-Role Agent Topology: Role Contracts & Boundaries (50 min) — The roles as the runtime really exposes them — verified MCP `prompts/list` (10 prompts), contract role fields, per-role tool boundaries, and the researcher→mapper→critic handoff as a sequence.
  • 01.4 The Autonomy Spectrum & The Sovereign Human Decision Gate (40 min) — Three concentric gates (critic, `humanCheckpoints`, `approvalReceipt`); the two phase enums kept straight; fail-closed phase advance demonstrated live.
  • 01.5 The Service Profit Chain: Linking Frontline Tooling to Customer Loyalty (45 min) — Heskett et al.'s seven links and Ch. 5's lead/lag measurement discipline, mapped to the `service-profit-chain` and `kpi-tree` artifact schemas — no metric, no link.
  • 01.6 Hands-On Sprint: Running Your First Agentic Service Discovery (55 min) — The complete canonical loop executed with real receipts: 8 commands, 3 deliberate fail-closed refusals, veto→pass, factcheck exit 0, deterministic renders, signed phase gate.

The ASD Platform & Toolchain

The @asd Runtime Engine, the MCP Server, and CLI Ergonomics

Deep-dive technical breakdown of the ASD runtime stack. Teaches the @asd package architecture (core schemas, engine execution, knowledge registry, render compilation), drives the same 29-tool surface over both the stdio MCP server (29 tools, 216 repo-corpus resources, 10 role prompts) and the standalone CLI, and covers deterministic SVG/HTML export into project renders/. DeepSeek Harness (dsh) is introduced honestly as a deferred integration surface with separate acceptance evidence.

View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 02.1 The Unified SDAP Runtime Stack — Core, Engine, Knowledge, Render, Report, Runtime (50 min) — Package layering of the monorepo suite, the capability registry, the Studio RPC boundary, and the verified canonical loop (project → evidence → contract → draft → gate → factcheck).
  • 02.2 One Runtime, Two Live Surfaces — CLI and MCP (DSH Studio as a Deferred Integration) (55 min) — Surface parity proven by driving one project through both transports; the DSH Studio pack (`exact-dsh-rc.5`, `@asd/ui` 0.5.0) read honestly as a deferred integration surface.
  • 02.3 The asd-mcp Server: 29 Tools, 216 Corpus Resources, 10 Role Prompts over stdio (55 min) — A live protocol session: initialize handshake, tools/list, paginated resources/list (100+100+16), resources/read and sd_knowledge corpus retrieval, prompts/get role contracts.
  • 02.4 Terminal Ergonomics — the asd CLI Grammar, Fail-Closed Errors, and the Workspace Layout (45 min) — The real grammar (`asd --flag value`), verbatim fail-closed errors as the manual, two phase enums not to conflate, and the per-project JSON workspace tree.
  • 02.5 The Deterministic Export Pipeline — Typed JSON to SVG and HTML, Byte for Byte (55 min) — Why LLMs never draw: `@asd/render`'s 32 per-kind SVG compilers, `@asd/report`'s six dossier kinds, verified byte-identical re-export.
  • 02.6 The Host Matrix, Honestly — Verified Profiles, Unverified Hosts, and the ACP Boundary (45 min) — claude-code/codex/kimi/opencode vendor the same server; agy/pi/omp are static; grok is prompt-only (generic MCP client); DSH/BUZZ deferred; ACP record-only and fail-closed.

Evidence Registers, Fact-Checking & Review Gates

SHA-256 Content-Addressed Evidence, The Critic Agent & Machine-Checkable Rigor

The rigor engine that prevents hallucinated AI design work. Master the core rule: 'Handoffs are typed artifacts, never chat'. Learn how content-addressed SHA-256 evidence registers store verbatim quotes, telemetry logs, and enterprise documents. Configure the adversarial Critic agent, manage review-veto-logs, and run deterministic sd_factcheck CI gates.

View all 5 modules & syllabus
  • 03.1 The Law of Artifacts: Why Conversational Memory Destroys Rigor (45 min) — Chat context is lossy, private, and non-replayable; typed artifacts on disk are the only auditable handoff. Includes the draft→final state machine and a fail-closed dangling-citation demo.
  • 03.2 SHA-256 Evidence Registers & Content-Addressed Storage (50 min) — `id = sha256(JSON([source, kind, body, origin | null]))` — proven live: identical quote → identical id (dedup), one character changed → different id (avalanche). The register is plain per-project JSON; no SQLite.
  • 03.3 The Evidence Taxonomy: Quotes, Telemetry & Documents (45 min) — Methodological triangulation as a closed kind set; `observation` rejected at the schema; the import pipeline probed live — workspace containment, strict UTF-8, hard `maxBytes` cap, `onError: fail|skip`.
  • 03.4 The Critic Agent: Adversarial Review & the Review-Veto-Log Lifecycle (50 min) — Full row schema (`rowKind`, `severity`, `message`, `rule?`, `fix?`, `evidenceId?`); veto requires defect+fix, pass requires an evidence citation; both verdicts run live with the persisted log artifact.
  • 03.5 Deterministic Fact-Checking & CI Gates with `sd_factcheck` (50 min) — The four fields (integrity / schema / acceptance / review), a real red report naming the failing rule, in-place remediation to green, and a GitHub Actions gate on the exit code.

Scaled Research Ops & Behavioral Personas

Mining 200+ Field Transcripts with Agent Swarms & Fusing Telemetry with Qualitative Need Clusters

Scale user research operations without sacrificing qualitative nuance. Ingest transcripts as typed evidence (sd_evidence_import), code them with registered methods (transcript-mining, aeiou-coding) into insight sets and affinity clusters whose every node cites its evidence, then generate behavioral personas that fuse qualitative quotes with telemetry — no demographic flat-mapping, no unregistered one-off tools.

View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 04.1 Scaled Qualitative Ingestion — From 200 Transcripts to a Typed Evidence Store (50 min) — Scaled ingestion as repeated typed adds + deterministic workspace-local CSV/transcript import; evidence-kind enum (`quote`/`telemetry`/`document`) and origin provenance; the `research-plan-design → discussion-guide-design → interview-guide` instrument chain.
  • 04.2 Coding at Scale — Open/Axial Coding with Verbatim Provenance (45 min) — KJ affinity and AEIOU coding as executable method contracts; provenance lives in `nodes[].evidence[]`, not timestamps; dangling-citation writes refused; acceptance failures retained as auditable drafts.
  • 04.3 Eliminating Bias with Structured Parallelism — Agreement Without Swarms (45 min) — Confirmation/single-coder/selection bias; inter-coder agreement as critic review rows (proposal/defect + fix + evidenceId); parallelism = independent method runs over one content-addressed store with idempotent rewrites. No swarm claims.
  • 04.4 The Demographic Fallacy & Behavioral Signatures (50 min) — Why demographic segments mislead service design; behavioral signatures as the persona's unit of construction; live `behavioural-personas → persona-set` run with a `forbid-field-values: demographic` acceptance rule.
  • 04.5 Fusing Telemetry with Qualitative Data — Triangulation on One Node (50 min) — Denzin's triangulation at citation granularity; bounded telemetry snapshots as typed evidence; mixed-kind citations on one node with the triangulation logic written into fields; contradiction as a finding.
  • 04.6 Machine-Checkable Personas — the Full Executable Spine (50 min) — The complete verified spine `method-run → artifact-write → review → factcheck → export-artifact`; all three refusal modes; deterministic SVG render; human-gated phase advance with approval receipts.

Construct living customer journey maps and Indi Young mental-model alignments anchored in immutable evidence. Encode emotion as quote-gated node fields, derive sentiment views honestly from those fields, map multi-channel touchpoints, identify failure demand, and execute in-place remediation as versioned artifact lineage — the living map is a versioned artifact chain, not a live-sync daemon.

View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 05.1 Mental Spaces vs. Feature Tracks: The Indi Young Paradigm (45 min) — Mental-model skylines above the alignment line, offerings below; `mental-model-diagramming` writes towers as nodes whose `belief` field is required and policed against interface vocabulary.
  • 05.2 Constructing Task Towers & Cognitive Ladders (50 min) — Task statements → towers → mental spaces; the three alignment states (support / half-way / counter-productive); `mental-model-alignment` builds two parallel journeys where divergence needs a quote from *both* sides.

  • 05.4 Deriving Dynamic Sentiment Curves from Verbatim Quotes (50 min) — Kalbach's valence coding (−5…+5) with an honest architecture: emotions are quote-gated node *fields*, the curve is a declared derived view, and the closed schema rejects smuggled numbers.

Master Service Blueprinting: 5 Lanes + the Orchestration Lane

The Executable Industry Standard for Service Architecture, Autonomous Lanes & Human Escalation Paths

The flagship course on the foundational artifact of service delivery. Master the 5 canonical lanes (Customer Actions, Physical Evidence, Frontstage Staff, Backstage Operations, Support Systems) and lines of demarcation (Visibility, Interaction, Penetration). Then extend the model with the real orchestration lane: sd_orchestration_plan/status/dispatch/handoff for agent-executed steps with idempotency keys, retry semantics, and human-gated escalation — no fantasy swarms.

View all 7 modules & syllabus
  • 06.1 Why Blueprints Are the Architecture of Service Delivery (45 min) — Shostack 1982/1984 and Bitner/Ostrom/Morgan 2008: why a blueprint is an operational specification, not an infographic, and why it is distinct from a journey map.
  • 06.2 The Five Canonical Lanes & Demarcation Boundaries (50 min) — The five-lane vocabulary as required schema data, plus a verified repairs-service swimlane blueprint authored in the runtime.
  • 06.3 Lines of Visibility, Interaction & Internal Penetration (45 min) — The three governing lines as data on edge crossings, not pixels on a canvas, and the governance consequences of each boundary.
  • 06.4 The Sixth Lane Done Right: Orchestration Plans & Human Gates (55 min) — The real sixth lane: `sd_orchestration_plan/status/dispatch/handoff`, retry policy, typed handoffs, and explicit human gates — not autonomous swarms.
  • 06.5 Confidence Thresholds, Error Handling & Human Escalation Paths (50 min) — Why escalation is implemented as review gates, veto logs, retryable failures, and signed phase approvals rather than a fake confidence router.
  • 06.6 Blueprints-as-Code: The Full Authoring Session (50 min) — A complete live session: evidence → journey map → blueprint with provenance → review → factcheck → export → backlog contract.
  • 06.7 Automated Blueprint Diagnostics, Validation & Bottleneck Audits (35 min) — `sd_factcheck` and `sd_review` as complementary validators, with live failure receipts for `min-nodes`, `min-evidence-per-node`, empty lane, placeholder lane, and dangling evidence.

Service Quality, Good Services Audits & Continuous Operations

Grönroos Perceived Quality, Lou Downe's 15 Principles Machine Audit, Service Recovery SLAs & Continuous Blueprints

The capstone course for enterprise service leaders. Implement Grönroos Perceived Quality, Parasuraman's 5 GAPs, and 3-tier KPI trees linking operational telemetry to financial ROI. Run Lou Downe's 15 Principles of Good Services as a machine-checkable experience-audit artifact. Engineer recovery playbooks that capitalize on the Service Recovery Paradox. Transition to continuous service architecture: evidence reopens loops, artifacts version, and phase gates stay human-owned.

View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 08.1 Grönroos Quality Management & The Five Service Gaps (50 min) — Total perceived service quality, technical vs. functional quality, and the five-gap model mapped directly onto ASD gates: evidence, acceptance, critic, factcheck, human approval.
  • 08.2 Three-Tier KPI Trees — From Operational Telemetry to Financial Outcome (45 min) — Service Profit Chain and Outside In measurement turned into real `kpi-tree` artifacts with baselines, targets, and causal edges from operational drivers to financial outcomes.

  • 08.4 Automated Standards Audit — the Continuous-Audit Loop (50 min) — `service-standard-authoring`, `experience-audit`, `good-services-audit-15`, critic review, and `sd_factcheck` wired into a CI-style continuous-audit loop.
  • 08.5 Service Recovery Engineering — Playbooks, Justice, and the Recovery Paradox (45 min) — Grönroos service recovery theory turned into `recovery-playbook` artifacts with explicit triggers, justice dimensions, and learning loops back into evidence.
  • 08.6 Continuous Service Architecture — Approval Gates, Re-Open Loops, and Living Ownership (50 min) — The living operating rhythm: project phases vs. method phases, approval receipts, rejected-key replay semantics, roadmap/RACI ownership, and evidence-driven re-versioning.
