01
Foundation

Agentic Service Design Fundamentals

The AI-First Service Design Operating System, 10-Agent Team Topology & Designer-Agent Loop

The foundation course for the whole curriculum. Replaces static sticky notes and workshop theater with an executable, AI-driven Service Design Operating System. Connects classical service theory (Vargo & Lusch's Service-Dominant Logic, Service Profit Chain) to modern agentic systems. Teaches the 10-role Eve-style agent team topology, the autonomy spectrum, and the sovereign human decision gate.

▶ View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 01.1 The Death of Workshop Theater: Moving to an Executable Service OS (45 min) — Why traditional sticky-note workshops produce static artifacts that decay upon project close, and how treating service design as an executable, file-backed operating system changes delivery velocity.
  • 01.2 Service-Dominant Logic & Value Co-Creation in the Age of AI (50 min) — Bridging Vargo & Lusch's SDL theory with autonomous agents: understanding agents as active operant resources that dynamically co-create value with human actors.
  • 01.3 The 10-Role Eve-Style Agent Topology: Role Contracts & Boundaries (50 min) — Dissecting the 10 specialized agent roles (Researcher, Synthesizer, Mapper, Ideator, Prototyper, Critic, Presenter, Measurer, Librarian, Orchestrator) and why strict role boundaries prevent cognitive drift.
  • 01.4 The Autonomy Spectrum & The Sovereign Human Decision Gate (40 min) — Establishing where agents operate with full autonomy (discovery, transcription, cross-referencing) versus where human designers maintain sovereign decision authority (phase gates, ethics, trade-offs).
  • 01.5 The Service Profit Chain: Linking Frontline Tooling to Customer Loyalty (45 min) — Operationalizing Heskett's Service Profit Chain in AI systems: how internal agentic capability directly drives frontline satisfaction, service value, and enterprise ROI.
  • 01.6 Hands-On Sprint: Running Your First Agentic Service Discovery (55 min) — An end-to-end hands-on tutorial running an agentic service discovery sprint from a raw problem brief to an evidence-backed initial diagnostic.

Foundation

The ASD Platform, Toolchain & DeepSeek Harness (dsh)

DeepSeek Harness Studio, the @asd Runtime Engine, MCP Server, and CLI Ergonomics

Deep-dive technical breakdown of the Service Design Agent Platform (SDAP) runtime stack. Explains how DeepSeek Harness (dsh) mounts the ASD Cordis plugin, how @asd/engine exposes 52 native tools, how the asd-mcp server bridges external clients over stdio with 215 knowledge resources, and how the asd CLI operates in terminal sessions. Covers deterministic SVG/HTML rendering.

▶ View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 02.1 The Unified SDAP Runtime Stack: Engine, Runtime, Render & Report (50 min) — Architecture of the monorepo suite: @asd/core schemas, @asd/engine execution, @asd/render compilation, and @asd/report generators.
  • 02.2 DeepSeek Harness (dsh) Studio Workbench & Cordis Plugin Wiring (55 min) — Mounting the ASD Cordis plugin inside DeepSeek Harness to unlock the visual Service Design Studio, live project dashboards, and artifact inspector.
  • 02.3 The asd-mcp Server: Exposing 52 Tools & 215 Knowledge Resources (55 min) — Running the stdio Model Context Protocol server, paginating knowledge URIs (asd://corpus/...), and registering tool schemas with LLM clients.
  • 02.4 Terminal Ergonomics with the asd CLI & Headless Scripting (45 min) — Executing scripted service design runs with the standalone asd CLI, handling project init, phase transitions, and headless batch operations.
  • 02.5 Deterministic Visualization: Compiling Structured JSON to Flawless SVG/HTML (55 min) — Why LLMs should never draw diagrams directly: compiling typed JSON models to zero-hallucination Mermaid, SVG, and responsive HTML artifacts.
  • 02.6 Multi-Host Distribution: Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, AGY & Pi (45 min) — Deploying the ASD plugin across 12 different agent runtime environments with unified role contracts and diagnostic verification.

Evidence Registers, Fact-Checking & Review Gates

SHA-256 Content-Addressed Evidence, The Critic Agent & Machine-Checkable Rigor

The rigor engine that prevents hallucinated AI design work. Master the core rule: 'Handoffs are typed artifacts, never chat'. Learn how content-addressed SHA-256 evidence registers store verbatim quotes, telemetry logs, and enterprise documents. Configure the adversarial Critic agent, manage review-veto-logs, and run deterministic sd_factcheck CI gates.

▶ View all 5 modules & syllabus
  • 03.1 The Law of Artifacts: Why Chat-Based Agent Handoffs Cause Toxic Drift (45 min) — Why passing conversational context across LLM subagents causes loss of fidelity, and how filesystem-first typed artifacts enforce mathematical integrity.
  • 03.2 Content-Addressed Evidence Registers: SHA-256 Cryptographic Provenance (50 min) — How every research quote, telemetry row, and document snippet is assigned an immutable SHA-256 hash that downstream artifacts must cite.
  • 03.3 The Evidence Taxonomy: Quotes, Telemetry, and Document Registers (45 min) — Deep dive into the three formal evidence kinds: verbatim qualitative quotes, quantitative operational telemetry, and policy/institutional documents.
  • 03.4 The Adversarial Critic Agent & The Review-Veto-Log Lifecycle (50 min) — How the Critic agent (kind 38) evaluates artifacts against machine rules, issues binding vetoes with mandatory fixes, and gates phase transitions.
  • 03.5 Deterministic Fact-Checking in CI/CD with sd_factcheck & Quality Gates (50 min) — Automating fact-checking in continuous integration: failing builds whenever an artifact references missing, altered, or hallucinated evidence hashes.

Scaled Research Ops, Mining & Behavioral Personas

Scale user research operations without sacrificing qualitative nuance. Ingest and synthesize hundreds of transcripts using parallel extraction swarms while eliminating confirmation bias. ▶ View all 6 modules & syllabus
  • 04.1 Scaled Qualitative Ingestion: Mining 200+ Field Transcripts & Inquiries (50 min) — Ingesting unstructured audio transcripts, field notes, and contextual inquiry logs into structured, token-budgeted research packets.
  • 04.2 Automated Coding, Thematic Tagging & Quote Provenance Preservation (45 min) — Deploying the Researcher agent swarm to perform grounded-theory open and axial coding while preserving speaker metadata and exact timestamps.
  • 04.3 Eliminating Researcher Bias with Parallel Extraction Swarms (45 min) — Running adversarial blind extraction swarms with randomized prompt framing to prevent confirmation bias and isolate contradictory user signals.

Master Service Blueprinting: 5-Lane Architecture to 6th-Lane Swarms

The Executable Industry Standard for Service Architecture, Autonomous Lanes & Human Escalation Paths

The flagship course on the foundational artifact of service delivery. Master the 5 canonical lanes (Customer Actions, Physical Evidence, Frontstage Staff, Backstage Operations, Support Systems) and lines of demarcation (Visibility, Interaction, Penetration). Then pioneer the 6th Lane: modeling autonomous AI agents, tool invocations, confidence thresholds, and human-in-the-loop escalation paths.

▶ View all 7 modules & syllabus
  • 06.1 Why Blueprints Are the Architecture of Service Delivery (45 min) — How service blueprints translate customer experience into operational specifications, organizational contracts, and technological requirements.
  • 06.2 The Five Canonical Lanes and Operational Demarcations (50 min) — Defining the rigid schema boundaries of Physical Evidence, Customer Actions, Frontstage Interactions, Backstage Actions, and Support Processes.
  • 06.3 Lines of Visibility, Interaction, and System Penetration (45 min) — Mapping the three critical lines that govern what the customer experiences, where human frontline touch occurs, and where deep infrastructure takes over.
  • 06.4 The Sixth Lane: Modeling AI Agent Swarms, LLM Decisions & Tool Calls (55 min) — Why traditional 5-lane blueprints collapse in AI-native organizations: introducing Lane 6 to model autonomous agent decisions, tool execution, and state.
  • 06.5 Confidence Thresholds, Error Cascades & Human-in-the-Loop Escalation Paths (50 min) — Engineering deterministic fallback loops, automated failovers, and graceful human handoffs when LLM confidence drops below threshold.
  • 06.6 Blueprints-as-Code: Authoring & Compiling with sd_blueprint (50 min) — Hands-on authoring of full typed blueprints, running schema verification, generating SVG visual layouts, and validating cross-lane alignments.
  • 06.7 Automated Blueprint Diagnostics, Validation & Bottleneck Audits (35 min) — Using the Critic and Measurer agents to perform automated bottleneck detection, orphaned system dependencies, and failure mode analysis on blueprints.

Service Quality, Good Services Audits & Continuous Operations

Grönroos Perceived Quality, Lou Downe's 15 Principles Machine Audit, Service Recovery SLAs & Continuous Blueprints

The capstone course for enterprise service leaders. Implement Grönroos Perceived Quality, Parasuraman's 5 GAPs, and 3-tier KPI trees linking operational telemetry to financial ROI. Automate Lou Downe's 15 Principles of Good Services with good-services-audit-15. Engineer automated recovery playbooks to capitalize on the Service Recovery Paradox. Transition to Continuous Service Architecture.

  • 08.2 Building 3-Tier KPI Trees: Operational -> Experiential -> Financial Traceability (45 min) — Constructing end-to-end measurement trees that prove the direct financial ROI of UX friction reduction and service improvements.
  • 08.3 Lou Downe's 15 Principles of Good Services: Decoded into Machine-Auditable Heuristics (50 min) — Deconstructing the definitive public service standard (Lou Downe) into structured rules checkable by automated agents.
