Writing and tooling

Personal projects from 2025 and 2026, separate from my employment work. I built them to think through how service design methods hold up when AI agents do the first draft, and to learn what it takes to ship a tool. They were written and coded with AI assistance, and their examples use fictional organisations, labelled as such.

What this is evidence of

And what it is not

This material shows that I can specify a method precisely enough for a machine to run it, that I can build and document a working tool with AI assistance, and that I care about evidence gates on generated output. It does not show production software delivery to users, a client engagement, or measured outcomes. The employment work under Work is the hiring evidence; this is the workshop behind it.
