The practice

The Agentic Service Designer

Service design, run through agents — method as code, evidence as currency, and the human designer as the judgment behind the wheel.

The idea

Service design produces reliable results only when its methods are executed with discipline — and discipline is exactly what standardised, versioned, reviewable procedures give an AI agent. An agentic service designer encodes each method (research-to-evidence, journey mapping, blueprints, persona building, HMW, prototyping) as a repeatable SOP with an output schema, acceptance criteria and a review gate, then runs the whole engagement through a small team of specialised agents.

⚡

AI-first practice

Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex and GitHub as the service designer’s working surface — agents do the reading, mining, drafting and mapping; the designer owns framing, decisions and client trust.

The tools

Four tools are the primary working surface of the practice. Each has a distinct role.

Claude Code

The orchestrator. Dispatches research, synthesis and mapping subagents, authors the artifacts, and runs the review loops.

OpenCode

The engineer. Agent-mode builds for data pipelines, mock dataset generation, prototypes and telemetry mining.

Codex

The critic and researcher. Deep research, adversarial review of the business case, and independent second opinions.

GitHub

The system of record. Issues as work items, pull requests as review gates, Actions enforcing mermaid validity and artifact schemas.

Method as code

The core pattern: an SOP is not a paragraph of advice — it is a prompt plus an output schema plus acceptance criteria plus a gate. Versioned in git, tested in CI, and reusable across engagements.

What stays human

The point of the practice is not to replace the designer. It is to remove the mechanical load so judgment has room. The agents read, mine, draft and criticise; the designer frames the problem, owns the client relationship, makes the calls, and carries the ethics.

· Framing

Reframing the brief and choosing what to study is abductive, political and irreducibly human.

· Judgment

Killing a beloved concept, scoping a pilot, negotiating a gate — decisions with consequences stay with people.

· Ethics

Coercion risk, data dignity, vulnerability, inclusion. No agent signs those off.

· Client trust

The relationship, the room, the workshop with real stakeholders — trust is not delegable.
