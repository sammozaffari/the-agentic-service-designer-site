The harness: ten agent roles, one review gate

A personal project: the working model behind The Agentic Service Designer. Ten agent roles share one practice of 74 service design methods, each encoded as a procedure with an output schema, acceptance criteria and a review gate, compiled into packs for ten agent hosts. It is a description of a working model built with AI assistance and fictional worked examples, not a record of employment or client work.

How work moves through it

A human frames the problem and sets the gates. The orchestrator plans and dispatches. The researcher, synthesiser and mapper turn evidence into typed artefacts that cite their sources. The ideator, prototyper and measurer take those artefacts into concepts, prototypes and measurement plans. The critic attacks every claim before an artefact can be marked final; the presenter and librarian package and keep provenance. Every artefact write runs machine-checked acceptance rules, and a fact-check replays every evidence citation before anything is published.

Orchestrator

Owns the lifecycle and the phase gates; turns intent into a plan of record and reports gates back to the human.

Researcher

Research plans, interviews and transcripts into an evidence register with sources attached.

Synthesiser

Insight records, personas and themes, each citing the evidence rows it was built from.

Mapper

Journey maps and service blueprints as typed artefacts with lanes, stages and fail points.

Ideator

Opportunity framing and concept generation against the map, with rejected alternatives recorded.

Prototyper

Prototypes and test plans at the fidelity the question needs.

Measurer

Baselines, candidate measures and pilot designs; refuses outcome claims without data.

Critic

Adversarial review on the write path. A method that declares a critic gate cannot be final without a verdict.

Presenter

Reports and decks generated from the artefacts, so every claim points back to a record.

Librarian

Method provenance against the service design canon; keeps the practice honest.

What is verified, counted from code

29 tools on the published MCP server, 10 agent roles, 74 method skills, 31 artefact kinds, 10 host packs, machine-checked acceptance rules, an evidence fact-check, a critic gate on the write path and a CI drift check that recompiles the packs. The platform manual documents the tools and the templates page holds the seven authored artefact templates.

What stays human

Framing the problem, choosing what to study, killing a concept, scoping a pilot, and the ethics of research with real people. The agents read, mine, draft and criticise. The designer decides.
