Executive read

One connected attrition chain explains the three original briefs. The strongest controllable levers are not pay: they are readiness before day one, availability-matched hours, visible progress and recognition during learning.

Headline evidence

The perception gap

Managers rate the same behaviours 25–35 points higher than crew do. The gap is not a measurement artefact — it is the operating model: the manager group is committed and overloaded, and the load transfers down the shift.
