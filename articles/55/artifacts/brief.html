The brief, as asked

The client is a global quick-service restaurant chain with hundreds of restaurants run as a mix of company-owned and franchisee-operated groups. The people function commissioned research with three questions:

  • How do current services enable the end-to-end team-member experience?
  • How is recruitment and onboarding enabled — deeper and more holistically?
  • What is expected of managers at each level, from shift supervisor to restaurant manager?

The mobilization purpose: inform system improvements that better deliver to restaurant team members, connect the team-member experience with the customer experience, shape a path forward, and deliver a manager capability framework that closes the top-priority gaps.

The real problem

Attracting and onboarding good people was an ongoing challenge, and the pain was structural. Systems were disconnected across channels; candidates and managers re-entered the same information into multiple systems; there was no reporting to inform recruitment performance; and processes were bypassed, with different franchisee groups following different processes.

The funnel quantified the leak: roughly 34,000 careers-site visitors a month, nearly half gone before an application was completed. More than nine in ten hires are crew roles, and more than nine in ten managers are promoted from within — the crew funnel is the sole feeder of the entire management pipeline. Assistant-manager turnover rose from 18% to 27% in four years, restaurant-manager turnover from 13% to 17%, compressing the six-plus-year tenure path from crew to restaurant manager. A growth plan of hundreds of new restaurants multiplied the demand on a process already straining.

Scope

In scope
  • End-to-end crew recruitment and onboarding across the network — nine journey stages, from first thought to first shift
  • Layered current-state blueprint with fail points, measures, and owners
  • Automation flow design for the application→interview core
  • Manager competency framework for the promotion-from-within pipeline
Out of scope
  • Franchisee technology configuration — the automation must work across the network without breaking any group’s setup
  • Support-office and above-store recruitment; manager recruitment (99%+ of volume is crew)
  • One-off recruitment campaigns as the answer to manager capability
  • Replacement of existing functional improvement projects

Constraints

AspectConstraint

Vendor surfaceThe recruiting platform is expensive to change, reports on request only, and offers no roadmap visibility or testing window before releases
Supply chainUniform and name-badge lead times outrun hiring
Data layerThe latest update is treated as correct; report history is capped at 90 days; no first-shift or 90-day retention data exists

Why service design

This was not a form-design problem on one web page. The leaks spanned a careers site, a recruiting platform, a work-rights verification service, payroll, time & attendance, a learning platform, uniform supply, restaurant managers, and the candidate’s own family — a socio-technical system whose failures live below the line of visibility. The reframed problem that anchored the engagement:

Intentionally design a recruitment and onboarding experience for new talent that attracts and retains the best candidates, streamlines manager activities, and drives great customer experiences.
