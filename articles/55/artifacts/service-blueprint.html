Reading the blueprint

The line of visibility sits between the candidate’s experience and the machinery that produces it. Work-rights verification stays backstage, but the candidate always knows the check is running and what to do if it stalls. The two empty cells — first-shift attendance and 90-day retention — are drawn as explicit no-data cells with owners, not omitted.

The re-hire branch is a first-class path: candidates who previously worked for the same franchisee group skip the work-rights check and onboarding and transfer their existing record — training and credentials carried over; everyone else runs the full workflow with new records.
