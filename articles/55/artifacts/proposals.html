The concept direction — Hire in Days, Not Weeks

The convergence call: automate the application→interview core first, because that is where the funnel bleeds and where the chain controls the surface. Five components, scored desirability 4.5 · feasibility 4.1 · viability 4.3 (DFV, 1–5):

AI assistant
Screens with five conversational questions — age, work rights, availability, shifts per week, high-demand shift availability — and answers candidate questions day or night.
Auto-scheduled interviews
High-fit candidates get an interview invitation in the conversation itself; reminders at 24/48/72 hours; unreachable candidates dispositioned after 10 days of no action.
Integrated work-rights verification
Automated document, biometric, and registry checks with a human review lane; guided capture for candidates without photo identification.
Automated employee-record setup
Employee record created at offer; two daily pushes to scheduling; payroll validation with full-pack push-back on failure.
Re-hire branch
Same franchisee group: skip work-rights and onboarding, transfer the record. Different group or first job: full workflow with new records.

Screening decision logic

RuleRoute
Minimum-age gateUnder-18 branch: parental-consent flow runs first; never disposition before consent is attempted
Work-rights flagAutomated checks; failure → human review lane → accept or reject with the reason emailed
High-demand shift = YES and 3–5 shifts per weekAUTO-SCHEDULED interview
Any other combinationAvailability in review — hiring-manager queue
Incomplete applicationReminders at 24/48/72 hours
No action after 10 daysDispositioned; the loop is closed

The logic was simulated against 1,000 seeded applicants before build, with the auto-scheduling rule held fixed even when the first pass showed only 61% of high-fit candidates confirming interviews — the fix was the reminder cadence, not a looser rule.

Rollout plan

PhaseTimelineContent
Simulationweeks 10–131,000 seeded applicants through the decision matrix; thresholds set before the run; the 61% first-pass confirmation rate drove the reminder cadence, not a looser rule
Pilot lanesweeks 12–14Two franchisee groups; manager walkthroughs; the review queue tuned so the oldest and borderline cases surface first
Go liveweeks 14–16Sequence: screen → schedule → verify → set up; weekly steering with the people function; compliance-locked rules enforced in CI
Measureweek 15+Dashboard pulls replace on-request reporting; in-flow surveys after interview, decline, and hire; the measurement gaps instrumented with owners

Safeguards & controls

  • Compliance locked. Anything touching age, parental consent, or work rights carries a compliance label; merge is blocked without people-function and compliance sign-off.
  • Human review lanes. Identity checks that cannot auto-confirm go to a person, with the reason given to the candidate.
  • Candidate data privacy. Agents work on pseudonymized transcripts and fixed-seed sample data only; consent, age, and work-rights flows follow data minimization.
  • Honest measurement. No first-shift or 90-day retention data exists yet — each gap is a documented finding with an owner and a target instrument.

Known friction, carried into the backlog

Document capture still fails for some candidates and requires a ticket; the manager review surface needs simplification; there is no bulk update, so candidates are actioned one by one; the platform date selector makes it easy to pick the wrong year. Each was logged as open work, not hidden.
