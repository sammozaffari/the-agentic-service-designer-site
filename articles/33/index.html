33 — The Virtual Ward at Capacity: Scaling Telehealth with the ASD Critic on Every Gate

00 · Provenance note

Confidentiality & provenance note

Halo Care is a fictional composite telehealth provider; no real patient, clinician, or outcome data appears. This article is a runlog of the Agentic Service Designer (ASD) in Claude Code. Plugin surface and behavior are the real platform; clinical-safety governance is implemented as critic verdicts persisted as review-veto-log artifacts, plus gate minutes the human clinical lead signs — not as an engine feature, because there is no engine feature — and the article says so plainly.

01 · Executive summary

Executive summary

A telehealth provider scaled from 400k to 1.4M patients and broke at the seams: 22-minute waits, 18% did-not-attend, 11 concurrent cases per clinician with no context handover, first-visit resolution 61%. One designer rebuilt the care journey in 20 weeks on the ASD plugin, with the Critic gating every review-gated artifact and clinical governance co-signing gate decisions. Virtual wait 22→6 min, DNA 18%→7%, first-visit resolution 61%→78%, clinician eNPS −10→+25.

ClientHalo Care (fictional composite)
Sector / serviceHealthcare; telehealth + virtual ward
Scope1.4M active patients, 12 regions, 480 clinicians
Duration20 weeks, 4 platform-gated phases
Team1 designer + 10 ASD role agents + 2 clinical-ops leads (0.5 FTE)
PlatformASD Claude Code plugin (platform/packs/claude-code), vendored MCP server, asd CLI
Headline resultsWait 22→6 min; DNA 18%→7%; resolution 61%→78%; eNPS −10→+25

What you will learn

  • How safety-critical gating runs on a platform whose engine has no "governance config": the critic's veto is a persisted artifact with severity-graded rows and mandatory fixes, and the clinical co-sign is a human minute filed next to it — that honesty is the contract.
  • The same method-skill surface as the other platform studies, applied to demand/capacity framing rather than field service.
  • Where clinician experience (books 19/20) becomes KPI rows with baselines.
02 · The brief

The brief

Halo Care won region after region on speed, then hit structural failure: the service was designed for a boutique model, and scaling meant either doubling clinical cost or redesigning demand. Leadership chose redesign with safety non-negotiable.

Success criteria (illustrative): virtual wait ≤ 8 min at peak; DNA ≤ 10%; first-visit resolution ≥ 75%; clinician eNPS ≥ +15; patient NPS ≥ +50; no safety-event trend increase. Constraints: clinical-governance sign-off on every route change; data protection; clinician scheduling protections; a hard budget cap on clinical hours; every redesign reversible per-region. Why service design: healthcare is a high-stakes service system (book 19); clinicians determine external quality (book 20); at scale the good/broken difference shows in hours (book 03).

The same engine runs bare in the terminal via platform/packs/cli/asd. Usage is asd <tool> [--flag value ...]; dashed aliases (asd project-create) and full tool names (asd sd_project_create) both work. asd help lists the flags per tool:

NOTE

  sd_project_create      name, client, sector, service, phase
  sd_evidence_add        projectId, source, kind, body
  sd_artifact_write      projectId, methodId, title, nodes, artifactId

What the engine enforces (each line verified against the built CLI; §13 reproduces every one):

  • Evidence is content-addressed and closed-kind. Rows get sha256 ids — same body, same id, in any workspace. Kind is exactly one of quote, telemetry, document; anything else is rejected at the boundary.
  • Writes are checked and always persisted. sd_artifact_write runs structural integrity plus the producing method's acceptance rules. A failure is not refused — it persists as status: draft with the check report attached, repairable in place via --artifactId. The one hard error is a dangling evidence ref: never persisted.
  • The critic sits on the write path. Every one of the seventy-four method manifests declares a critic review gate, so a method artifact reaches final only through sd_review: pass promotes; veto demotes to draft and persists the rows as a review-veto-log artifact (itself final, linked to its subject by reviewOf). Rows are {rowKind: defect|proposal, severity: critical|high|medium|low, message, rule?, fix?, evidenceId?}; a veto needs at least one defect row, and every defect row needs a fix.
  • The fact-check is deterministic. sd_factcheck replays integrity, acceptance, and review over every artifact in the project and exits 1 on any failure — a gate CI can consume.

Two things stayed human by convention, and the engagement recorded them as such: (1) clinical governance — a gate decision is a minute signed by the clinical lead, filed next to the critic's log id; the platform's part is the persisted veto log, and it never pretends to be a clinical authority; (2) the honest boundary — demand models, wait-time statistics, and capacity analysis are the team's analysis of the register and the raw data, marked as such, never attributed to the engine.

Gate G1. Register in place; critic over the research plan: no veto; one medium-severity proposal (night-shift sample) adopted. The clinical lead's minute sits next to the gate in the engagement repo.

Human judgment. The designer pushed clinician shadow days over convenience interviews, framed burnout as a safety metric (books 19/20) in front of the clinical lead, and rejected two praise-only transcripts (selection for insight).

The triage ladder — four demand classes with four access promises — was drafted into the urgent journey's queue routing, and that is where the critic met it. The veto, trimmed from the real return:

DATA

  "fields": {
    "rowKind": "defect",

    "message": "The queue node shows a single routine path while the telemetry row reports p90 41 min at peak; routing symptom class 3 as routine contradicts the register",

The row also carries the telemetry row's id as its evidence ref. A defect without a fix never reaches the log — the tool rejects it at the boundary (asd sd_review: rows[0]: a defect row requires a "fix"), because the rows are how the owning role remediates. Governance upheld the veto; the ladder was re-derived, re-gated, and the gate minute recorded the critic's log id next to the clinical lead's signature.

Human judgment. The designer's frame — "we do not schedule visits; we deliver access matched to acuity" — became the reference every later artifact cited.

4.3 Session 3 — Develop (weeks 10–16)

Drive.

NOTE
@mapper run orchestration-canvas and service-blueprinting, then
evidence-gated-blueprint over the care journeys.
@prototyper walk the warm handoff, the clinician context service, and the
escalation path as storyboard-walkthrough artifacts: assumption, fidelity,
subjects, pass/fail criteria.
@critic review each before governance co-signs the gate.

What the plugin did. The blueprint's five lanes and the evidence-gated acceptance rules — at least 5 nodes, every node citing the register, no lane left unknown/tbd/n/a — held the operational design honest: a route that silently reverted to the old 5-day queue was vetoed at review and corrected before the gate. The warm-handoff prototype went through the storyboard discipline: assumption, fidelity, subjects, pass/fail criteria.

Team analysis (outside the engine, marked). The handoff timing check — the 30-second protocol that would run 90 seconds at scale — came from the designer's scheduling analysis (with the clinicians), and the 60-second decision-tree protocol was the team's fix. The platform did not simulate.

Human judgment. The designer made the 8-minute wait promise stick against marketing's "as fast as possible," insisted the context service respect consent, and chaired the governance reviews where clinical and design language met.

07 · SOPs for AI

SOPs for AI — skills as the encoded method

The encoding is inspectable, not vibes: each method is a manifest — steps with role + instruction, machine-checkable acceptance rules, an output kind, and the critic review gate — plus a generated SKILL.md the agents load. Before writing anything, sd_method_run returns the contract the result will be judged against. The audit skill's, trimmed from the real return:

DATA
{
  "method": { "id": "good-services-audit", "phase": "measurement" },
  "steps": [
    { "role": "researcher",
      "instruction": "Score the live service against each heuristic in the good-services checklist, citing evidence for every score below target." },
    { "role": "critic", "gate": true,
      "instruction": "Reject any below-target score with no cited evidence." }
  ],
  "acceptance": [
    { "rule": "min-nodes", "min": 3 },
    { "rule": "min-evidence-per-node", "min": 1 },
    { "rule": "field-non-empty", "field": "theme" }
  ],
  "outputKind": "insight-set",
  "availableEvidence": 4
}

For safety-critical work the load-bearing parts: referential integrity is absolute (a cited row that does not resolve is a hard error at write, never persisted — a clinical route hinges on it); status is draft→final through the critic's verdict, never by writing harder; a veto demotes with rows — severity, rule, optional evidence, and a mandatory fix — persisted as a review-veto-log that outlives the conversation. Governance is a human gate minuted next to the critic's log; the platform never pretends to be a clinical authority.

Failure modes observed: (1) the median-only demand reading was wrong — analyst-corrected, and the register itself stayed because the rows were never wrong, the reading was; (2) a persona cited a nonexistent row — integrity hard error at write, never persisted; (3) an over-broad escalation threshold — critic veto with a fix row; (4) a below-target good-services score with no cited evidence — the method's own critic step vetoed it.

09 · AI tool playbook

AI tool playbook

Claude Code + ASD plugin — the engagement itself: /asd to plan and dispatch, role agents for the work, skills as the encoded methods, the vendored MCP server underneath. The critic gate was the starring mechanic. Failed at: safety judgment — relational risk stays with the clinical lead. asd CLI — the same engine bare in the terminal: deterministic cross-checks, the veto/repair/pass loop, exports, and the exit-coded fact-check that ran in CI. MCP server — what Claude Code actually calls: the 16 tools, the corpus resources, the role prompts; a self-contained vendored bundle (server + knowledge corpus) that other MCP hosts wire to directly — the manual install does exactly that through a project-root .mcp.json. GitHub — issues as work items, PRs as artifact gates, the fact-check's exit 1 blocking merge.

10 · Lessons learned

Lessons learned & pitfalls

  1. Median reporting hid the spike; model the distribution, not its center.
  2. Clinician burnout is a clinical-safety metric (books 19/20); that framing unlocked redesign.
  3. Governance on the path beats governance after it; the mechanism was the critic's persisted veto log filed next to a signed human minute.
  4. Prototype timing with load; the 60s handoff survived because it was stress-checked.
  5. A rollout rule the humans run beats a steering-committee promise; it caught and then explained the anomaly.
  6. Consent boundaries are design constraints; the context service is smaller because of them, and better.
