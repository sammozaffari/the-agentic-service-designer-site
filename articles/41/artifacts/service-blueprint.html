Physical evidence — what the customer keeps, sees or is handedN001–N020 · 20 notes

N001Store fascia carries the charcoal-grill flame mark across all 2,000 stores; 68 flagship grill-houses add a lit chimney motif. Field check found 214 sites with faded fascia vinyl past its 5-year replacement cycle — flagged to maintenance, not to branding, so nobody owns it.
N002The Copperline app icon and app-store listing are the first evidence for the 26% app-plus-delivery mix. Listing screenshots lag the current menu by up to two LTO cycles; Hana Kim's team updates them manually because there is no content pipeline from Flamebook to the store listing.
N003Digital menu boards refresh nightly at 03:00 from the CopperPOS v7 price file. Where the overnight sync fails (0.8% of store-nights), the board shows yesterday's prices and the till charges today's — the single most common source of till-level complaints in the call-centre logs.
N004Queue stanchions in standard stores lay out one serpentine for till + kiosk. Food-court sites (389) have no stanchions at all, so the queue bleeds into mall walkways and security guards informally marshal it during the 11:30–13:30 peak.
N005Drive-thru pre-order boards sit 18–24 m ahead of the speaker post at all 251 drive-thru express sites. Dwell on the board averages 26 s; boards double as the only place lane pricing is visible, which is why price complaints cluster when the board sync (N003) fails.
N006Order confirmation displays at kiosk and speaker post show the basket for 5 s before auto-dismiss. Field timing shows customers need 7–9 s to verify a 4-item basket; the truncation drives a measurable share of the 8.6% order-error rate (accuracy 91.4%).
N007Every receipt carries a QR into VoiceBoard; scan rate is 2.9%. Receipt paper is thermal and fades in roughly 11 weeks, so recovery claims referencing old receipts arrive unverifiable — call-centre agents are told to accept claims within 30 days only.
N008Dine-in customers receive a coaster buzzer at payment; standard stores carry a pool of 24, flagships 60. Buzzer loss runs 3 per store per month and replacements are ordered through StockBeak as a "front-of-house consumable", competing for budget with packaging.
N009Foil-lined wrap buckets in three SKUs (quarter, half, family) are the signature evidence of a dine-in tray. The bucket is deliberately over-engineered for heat retention so the car journey home does not degrade the grill char — the packaging program's one unambiguous win (N185).
N010Delivery orders leave in tamper-sealed insulated bags; the seal is applied on the courier shelf in customer view at food-court sites but behind the window at drive-thru express sites. Inconsistent seal visibility shows up in VoiceBoard themes as "was my bag opened?" — 41 mentions in the 90-day extract.
N011The window handover tray and the "check your bag before you merge" exit sign are the last drive-thru evidences.
