Provenance — read first

Fictional composite engagement. Every person, quote, figure and outcome is synthetic. No real company, store, or person is described.

What this artifact is

Two instruments run alongside the 8 flagship interviews: a 14-question store-manager survey (fielded weeks 2–4, 11–31 May 2026) and a 12-question crew survey (fielded weeks 3–5, 18 May–5 June 2026). Instruments are shown first, then results with distributions and derived statistics. Verbatim open-text extracts are numbered into the engagement evidence ledger — this file defines notes 271–300; do not renumber. Channel telemetry (VoiceBoard, POS, shopper, aggregator, call-centre) sits in channels.html; synthesis in insight-register.html and service-blueprint.html.

Methodology note

Sampling logic. Managers: a census of all 68 flagship GMs (41 responded) plus stratified random samples within each of the four remaining formats, stratifying proportionally to estate counts and balancing company/franchise ownership; franchise managers were reached through Flamebook, company managers through the ShiftOrbit manager console. Crew: census invitation to all flagship crews plus stratified random samples of store crews by format, weighted post-stratification to the format and tenure mix of the 41,300-strong crew population; kiosk and travel-hub crews received a paper route to avoid biasing against low app usage.

Precision. Manager survey: n=412, margin of error ±4.6% at 95% confidence (adjusted for stratification and a modest design effect). Crew survey: n=1,893, margin of error ±2.2% at 95% confidence. Subgroups (routed questions, tenure bands) carry wider error and are reported as indicative.

Known biases. Manager self-reports of compliance timing (Q3) run slightly optimistic against Q-Pulse telemetry; crew responses on C8 are self-incriminating and likely conservative; open-text coding was single-coder with a 10% second-coder audit (agreement 88%). All figures are synthetic and exist to exercise the pipeline, per the provenance note.
