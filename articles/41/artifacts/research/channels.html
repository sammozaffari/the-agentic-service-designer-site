Signals for synthesis

  • Accuracy, not speed, is the number-one customer signal — pair with shopper accuracy data when sizing the recovery ladder.
  • Cold-food growth is a delivery-chain metric in disguise; treat it as a handover problem (proposal: One Copperline Handover), not a kitchen problem.
  • LTO launch months are visible in the feedback stream: build a launch-readiness gate into the Franchise Ops Bridge proposal.
  • Channel skew: negative-only response behaviour (per interview 06) means volumes measure annoyance, not satisfaction — never read VoiceBoard as NPS.

Signals for synthesis

  • Perception gap quantified: measured 342s vs experienced 10–20 minutes — blueprint must model the queue, not just the kitchen.
  • Drive-thru 180 is a redesign of eleven 4-second losses (per interview 02), not one big delay; target the payment dip and hold-bin sequencing first.
  • Kiosk abandonment is cheap recovery volume; bundle with the accessibility remediation business case.
  • Modification handling is the highest-yield accuracy fix in the data — feed directly into the blueprint's order-taking lane.

  • Score-by-format gradient follows staffing density — blueprint should treat crew-per-shift as a frontstage constraint variable.

  • Promise overstatement ("the board said 8") converts recoverable waits into trust damage; quote honest times at peak.
  • Shopper evidence of courier invisibility independently corroborates courier telemetry and interviews.

Signals for synthesis

  • One handover standard (rack, label, honest ready-ping) addresses three complaint codes at once — cold food, late delivery, wrong order.
  • Flagships are the worst wait environment: pilot the handover standard at a flagship where the pain (and the brand exposure) is maximal.
  • The ready-ping is the cheapest fix in the whole data pack: change what the tablet claims, and perceived lateness falls without touching the kitchen.
  • Wheelio's 4.2 sits at the visibility threshold franchisees describe — rating-driven discovery makes breakage a commercial, not operational, problem.

Signals for synthesis

  • Treat the contact centre as a defect sensor: its reason codes are a live map of store and handover failures.
  • Join VoiceBoard text, call reason codes and POS voids on order ID — all three describe the same 91.4% accuracy problem from different altitudes.
  • Escalation and repeat contact both collapse if refund cycle time drops below 24 hours and band authority moves down one level.
  • Log the counter comps: the cheapest, fastest recovery tier is currently invisible to the business case.
