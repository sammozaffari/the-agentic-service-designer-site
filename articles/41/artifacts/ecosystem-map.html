3. Dependency risks

DR1Single-DC reliance, Harbourgate region. Every Harbourgate-region store is served by exactly one DC (F03). A one-day outage removes replenishment for the whole region with no reroute protocol. Proposal link: contingency annex of the blueprint (service-blueprint.html).
DR2Aggregator concentration + single integration layer. Dashly, Wheelio and Pedalr together carry the full 26% app + aggregator mix, and all three ride one Route56 layer (F10, F12). Ratings live on their platforms, so brand reputation is partly rented.
DR3LTO supply spikes. Limited-time-offer launches spike demand past supplier lead time (F01), driving the stockout/waste whiplash recorded in channel data. Proposal link: P5 Waste-to-Order Forecasting.
DR4Labour flywheel + peak concentration. 94% annualised crew turnover (F14) meets 62% of volume in two peak windows (F08); grill downtime (F18) compounds both. This is the ecosystem's engine of instability. Proposal link: P1, P4.
DR5Franchise playbook drift. Flamebook versioning lets partners run superseded playbooks (F06), so 780 stores diverge silently from the 1,220 company standard. Proposal link: P7 Franchise Ops Bridge.
DR6No shared courier handover standard. Three aggregators impose three pick-up rituals (F13); the 0.7% breakage rate is the visible cost of the missing standard. Proposal link: P3 One Copperline Handover.
DR7Compliance data integrity. 22% of Q-Pulse checks logged in bulk at close (F16) means the regulator-facing record misstates when food-safety checks actually happened. Proposal link: P6 Q-Pulse Honest Logging.

5. Reading notes

The centre is heavier than it looks. The Copperline core owns all seven named systems; every flow of data in the ecosystem terminates in one of them. That concentration is why Blueprint-as-Living-Document (P10) treats the systems layer as the re-baseline point.

Money flows in, labour flows out. Seven flows carry money toward or within the core; only F13–F15 describe what the ecosystem asks of people. The asymmetry — 3 labour flows against 7 money flows — is the map-level version of the crew voice gap.
