Quadrant counts. Manage closely: 11 (rows 1–8, 17, 18, 20). Keep satisfied: 4 (15, 19, 24, 25). Keep informed: 6 (9, 12, 13, 14, 16, 23). Monitor: 4 (10, 11, 21, 22). Total: 25 stakeholders.

Why the union sits left of centre. Wren Talbot currently holds medium interest (roster notice is a live topic) but can move to high power fast if roster stability slips again — the map is re-baselined when proposals land, per Blueprint-as-Living-Document (P10).

Aggregators are three stakeholders, not one. Dashly, Wheelio and Pedalr are plotted separately because One Copperline Handover (P3) has to clear three different contract positions — treating "aggregators" as one block was the failure mode in earlier programmes.
