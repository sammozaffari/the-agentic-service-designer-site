2. Method & annotation notes N366–N385

NoteContent
N366Affinity pass 1 — full interview corpus (8 flagship interviews + 17 stakeholder interviews) coded into first-order themes.
N367Affinity pass 2 — open-end responses from the 412-manager and 1,893-crew surveys coded against pass-1 themes.
N368Triangulation rule — an interview claim is only graded High when a survey or channel datum points the same way.
N369Confidence rubric — High: 2+ independent methods agree; Med: one method plus a supporting metric; Low: hypothesis with weak signal, kept for testing.
N370Minority-flag rule — contradicting evidence is kept and noted, never smoothed away (e.g. managers who defend manual overrides, see INS-23).
N371Insight compression workshop — Synthesizer + Mapper + Critic reduced ~140 coded observations to 24 register rows; discarded rows archived, not deleted.
N372Proposal mapping — each INS row bound to its P1–P10 response; rows serving two proposals carry both (INS-19, INS-20, INS-21, INS-22).
N373Wording rule for INS-08 — "bulk at close" behaviour described as falsified timing (canon term), never as individual dishonesty; systemic cause framing agreed with Aisha Rahman.
N374Segment-split rule — aggregate canon metrics must be split by format or channel before an insight claims a cause (applied in INS-20, INS-21).
N375Cost-of-inaction annotations drafted for the executive analysis; figures held in analysis-report.html, not duplicated here.

N377Flywheel causal-chain check — roster→attrition→instability loop verified against 3 independent data cuts before INS-01 was graded High.
N378Time-budget decomposition for INS-04 — 246s median window time split into grill wait, staging wait, and transaction; grill wait dominates the 66s gap.
