Reading journey B

Grace's Tuesday never rises above neutral — the curve is flat-negative by design of the current system, and its two floors are both canon metrics: an unstaffed peak (62% of volume in two windows) and a break system that exists on the roster but not on the floor. The final stage is the flywheel in miniature: the swap she cannot complete tonight is the same instability (11 requests/store/week, 31% rework) that will make next week's roster late, feeding the 94% annualised turnover. Stages 1 and 8 are P1's before-and-after; stage 7 is P6; stage 5 is P1+P4 (coverage-aware rosters, crew voice).
