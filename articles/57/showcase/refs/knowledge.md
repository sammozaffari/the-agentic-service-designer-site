# Pattern references: Knowledge base

Searched on Mobbin on 17 September 2026 before the screen was built. Task: the plain-language guide the research proposed, as a product surface.

| Pattern | Reference | Taken | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Help library with search first and a category rail | [Amazon, Help topics](https://mobbin.com/screens/29ee3038-b4ed-4679-b1b8-8effef391215) | A search field above everything with a placeholder phrased as a real question; a left topic rail with the current topic held open; the content column on the right | The two-column card grid, which buries the article a reader came for |
| Help centre with article counts and an owner byline | [Intercom, Help Center](https://mobbin.com/screens/dfa39e1e-71de-4850-8459-c9fe4e5da2da) | An article count against each topic, and a byline that says who keeps the page current | The illustrated hero band, which costs a screen of height and says nothing |
| Plain icon-led topic list, one per row | [Revolut, How can we help](https://mobbin.com/screens/fe3a659f-b364-40ca-b5ea-04851fa48533) | One topic per row with a small icon and no imagery, so the list scans in a second | Mobile-only scale and the back-arrow chrome |
