# The team member's app, pattern references

Searched on Mobbin before each screen was drawn. Most reporting flows on Mobbin are
built for a customer with a complaint; this one is for someone standing at a fryer
with one free hand, so the rejections matter as much as the borrowings.

| Pattern | Reference | Taken | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frontline home: one dominant action, generous spacing, today's work underneath | [Jobber, iOS home](https://mobbin.com/screens/6b7ee6db-6399-4183-9696-c06bb51ca166) | A single full-width primary sitting above everything else; a three-item tab bar; section labels in small caps over card groups | The onboarding to-do list, which turns a work tool into a checklist of the vendor's asks |
| Shift context carried in the header rather than asked for | [Grab Driver, booking planner](https://mobbin.com/screens/a2a5d8de-302d-47a0-9c99-ec6ccbc0ac54) | Status dot plus shift window plus who is on with you, as one quiet row | The date strip, which a restaurant shift does not need |
| The status of your own last request, on the home screen | [Hers, home with a request in review](https://mobbin.com/screens/e36b5e4e-8334-43c8-a1a4-4f71ea9f8043) | One outcome card above the routine, in plain words, with a tick rather than a progress bar | The promotional stack beneath it |
| Camera step with the instruction on the frame | [Bird, reporting an issue](https://mobbin.com/flows/4bb0fda2-0003-46b3-b0e0-9ab39d7a7721) | A white instruction card floating over a dark viewfinder, crop marks, and one oversized shutter with upload and skip either side | The uppercase letter-spaced option pills, which read as shouting in a safety context |
| Tracking your own submission through named steps | [7-Eleven, order status](https://mobbin.com/flows/4d23bdc2-dde5-4f4b-bfb7-e76c70049093) | A four-node progress line with a label under each node, ticks behind, a filled current node and greyed future steps | The estimated-time window, which would be a promise the service cannot keep |
| A calm confirmation that names what happened | [Forest, reporting damage](https://mobbin.com/flows/583931fc-466d-4805-a858-7b5d04b4882b) | The confirmation as a message from a person rather than a system toast, and a plain statement of what happens next | The full-width green bar, which disappears before it is read |
| The threshold question answered in the reader's own words | [Intercom, Help Center](https://mobbin.com/screens/dfa39e1e-71de-4850-8459-c9fe4e5da2da) | A short article row with a read time and a read count, surfaced where the hesitation happens | Search-first layout, which belongs on the manager's knowledge base, not the team member's home |
| Recognition that counts outcomes, not volume | [Airtasker, tasker dashboard](https://mobbin.com/screens/e8c2be58-e311-41ff-b80a-14963418a6f5) | The idea of a single headline figure with its composition written underneath | Tiers, badges and a personal score, which is the incentive the safety team rejected |
