# Pattern references: Incident record

Searched on Mobbin on 17 September 2026 before the screen was built. Each row records the pattern, the reference, what was taken and what was deliberately left behind.

| Pattern | Reference | Taken | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Named stage tracker across the top of a record | [Employment Hero, reference check lifecycle](https://mobbin.com/screens/4852a8e5-f3d8-44fc-97ac-f94fd950b7f4) | A horizontal tracker where every stage is named and carries its own state and owner, so the manager can see what has happened and what is theirs to do next | The lifecycle table underneath that repeats the tracker row for row. One statement of the stages is enough |
| Incident record with a pipeline, a timeline and a facts rail | [incident.io, incident record](https://mobbin.com/screens/ee853ddc-1292-42c1-9475-4b6703f8a7d7) | Stage pipeline in the header; the account of what happened and the activity in the main column; who and where in a right-hand rail | The Slack channel, post-mortem and duration-metrics chrome. A restaurant manager is on the floor, not in a war room |
| Properties rail beside the record body | [Linear, issue with properties panel](https://mobbin.com/screens/2e6c00de-40a7-455d-875f-0d8cb27c8270) | One line per fact in the rail, labels left and values right, so the facts can be scanned without reading the body | The keyboard-first density. Every control here has to work with a thumb on a phone in a kitchen |
| Linked work as its own section | [Jira, work item with linked items](https://mobbin.com/screens/e06dbe4d-6a5d-4808-94f1-8ce7fa26f9d1) | Corrective actions shown on the record as linked items with owner, due date and state, so the report and the fix stay joined up | Three levels of tabs, and the activity feed split across Comments, History and Work log |
| Timeline of what happened to the record | [Twenty, record timeline](https://mobbin.com/screens/c1b8ca68-1790-4df2-a715-1dea20b4b063) | A plain chronological list, newest work last, each entry naming the person | Field-by-field change logging, which buries the two events a manager cares about |
