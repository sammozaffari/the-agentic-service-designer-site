Incident or hazard? How to tell

Both go to the same place, so the word never stops you reporting. This page is here for the moment you want to be sure.

Something happenedAn incident. Someone was hurt, or nearly was.
  • I burned my hand on the fryer
  • A customer slipped in the dining room
  • A box fell off the top shelf and hit Sam
A condition that could cause harmA hazard. Nobody is hurt yet.
  • The floor is wet at the back door
  • The fire exit is blocked
  • The splash guard is missing on fryer 2

What if I am not sure?

Report it as whatever it looks like to you. Your manager works out the classification from what actually happened, not from your guess. Picking the wrong one is not a mistake you can make here.

What happens after you report

  1. Your manager is told straight away and checks that you are alright.
  2. They record what happened and how serious it turned out to be.
  3. Anything that needs fixing becomes an action with an owner and a due date.
  4. You are told what changed because you reported it.
