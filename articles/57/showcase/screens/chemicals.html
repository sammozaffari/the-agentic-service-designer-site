Wear

First aid, in plain words

On skin
Take off wet clothing. Rinse under running water for 15 minutes.
In eyes
Eyewash station in the chemical store (marker 6). Rinse for 15 minutes, hold the eyelids open, then call 13 11 26.
Swallowed
Do not make them vomit. Rinse the mouth, then call 13 11 26.

If it spills

  1. Keep people away. Put on gloves and goggles before you touch anything.
  2. Soak it up with the spill kit by the chemical store door (marker 12), then bag and label the waste.
  3. Tell the manager and report it as a hazard so the cause gets fixed.
