Future concept

Why voice: the report should fit the moment

Staff at a service counter often have no free hand and no phone on them. A voice path lets a report start the moment an incident happens, rather than at the end of a shift when the details have gone soft.

  • Severity first, same four levels, same meaning.
  • Reads the report back before it is filed, so the reporter can correct it.
  • Works hands-free and screen-free, so a phone ban never blocks a report.
  • Deliberate speed for critical events: it never prompts for detail during an emergency.

Voice is an addition, not a replacement. Every spoken option is also a visible, labelled, keyboard-reachable button, so the interface stays usable with no sound and no speech recognition.
