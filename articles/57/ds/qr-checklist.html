Manager onboarding checklist

QR-triggered, standardised training · removes inconsistent onboarding

Component · QR onboarding checklist
Scan at the time clock
1Scan the QROpens the 2-minute manager guide on any device.
2Learn severity, not typesHow bad first, then what happened. One short video.
3File your first test reportPick a real near miss so the flow is familiar before it matters.
4Know the no-device pathIf a phone is banned on the floor, the report can be filed at the register or handed to a manager.
5Sign off on the shiftConfirms training is complete and recorded for your store.

Steps are anchored by numbers and text, so the checklist reads in order without colour. Contrast is at least 4.5:1. Focus is visible on every interactive element. Each step has a short plain-language explanation.
