Concept prototype, built September 2026 from the December 2025 research Back to the case

What you are looking at. The incident and hazard reporting flow the safety research proposed, working, with its states. The research found that the live form asked the reporter two things they could not know in the moment: what the outcome of the injury would be, and whether the event was notifiable to the regulator. This design asks what happened in the reporter's own words and moves severity and the regulatory question to the manager's review, after the welfare check.

Use the tabs to compare the current form with the proposal, then walk the incident flow, the hazard flow and the manager's review. Field names in the current form are generalised from the research notes; the vendor's product is not reproduced.
