~200mobile-app support tickets, six months

Four methods triangulated on the same service: interviews spanning crew through area leaders, IT support, people-systems, and payroll; a role-branched survey with six branch paths; site visits and contextual inquiry at restaurants across equity and franchise groups; and a support-queue analysis of the mobile-app ticket cluster. The methods converged on the same top issues — live labour data, mobile parity, leave automation, pay-period corrections — which is what made each insight defensible.

Sampling signal

The survey’s area-leader and franchisee branches returned zero responses. That is a sampling signal, not a finding — those roles do not fill in surveys. The triangulation rule (every role covered by at least two methods) caught it, and the above-restaurant interview round re-covered both roles. The zero-response branches became a documented method lesson, not a silent gap in the evidence.
