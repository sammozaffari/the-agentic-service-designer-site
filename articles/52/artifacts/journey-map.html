Reading the maps

The emotion row is the story: the working week stays flat or negative wherever crew are blind to their own data — break timing, punch history, pay. Every fail marker traces to a blueprint fail point (F-01…F-08) with a named owner. Journey 2 was walked through with stakeholders as a validation device: it confirmed the pay-correction use case and turned the phantom-shift workaround into a first-class design brief (D2 — make the compliant path cheaper than the workaround).
