01 · Executive summary

Executive summary

A hydraulic access vendor with 41,000 machines in the field earned 31% of service revenue from aftersales add-ons. In this illustrative scenario, one designer re-architects services around outcome contracts in 22 weeks on the ASD operating model: Researcher builds the register, Synthesizer/Mapper frame the opportunity, Measurer wires the KPI set, Critic gates every review-gated artifact. All outcome figures are illustrative: service revenue share 31%→48%, renewals 71%→88%, MTTR 5.2→2.9 days, first-time fix 61%→79%, technician utilization 58%→71%.

02 · The brief

The brief

Aurora Vento: number-two vendor of hydraulic access platforms; hardware margin eroding; services a profit pool trapped by its own processes (74% manual work orders, remote parts availability 81%, MTTR 5.2 days). Anchor customers put availability guarantees on the table; leadership's instinct was pricing, the constraint was the service system.

Success criteria (illustrative, contract-shaped): availability ≥ 98% on pilots; MTTR ≤ 3 d; first-time fix ≥ 75%; technician utilization ≥ 68%; renewal ≥ 85%; services share ≥ 45%. Constraints: no hardware redesign; two-year rollout; legacy ERP; collective agreements limiting remote interventions; regional data protection on telemetry. Why service design: the offer is a value proposition in use (book 12) and the delivery chain runs through the technicians who are the brand (book 20).

The plugin, at a glance

3.1 Install

From the pack README (platform/packs/claude-code/README.md), verbatim:

TERMINAL

The marketplace manifest is platform/packs/.claude-plugin/marketplace.json; the plugin manifest is the pack's .claude-plugin/plugin.json. MCP wiring is the plugin-root .mcp.json, which Claude Code loads for installed plugins automatically; ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT} resolves inside the installed plugin, so the vendored server under mcp/ needs no PATH entry and no sibling checkout. 3.5 Status, gates, and what the machine actually enforces

Evidence rows are content-addressed (sha256 ids) with exactly three kinds: quote, telemetry, document — any other kind is rejected at the argument parser. Every sd_artifact_write runs structural integrity (dangling evidence refs are hard errors) plus the method's acceptance rules (min-nodes, min-evidence-per-node, field-non-empty). A failing write is still persisted as status: draft with its check failures attached — rejection shapes the record, it does not erase it. sd_review then records the Critic's verdict: pass flips an accepted draft to status: final; veto demotes it (back) to draft and writes a final review-veto-log artifact linked to its target by reviewOf. sd_factcheck replays integrity + acceptance + review state over every artifact in the project and exits 1 on any failure. Phase gates themselves are human decisions at the phase boundary, taken on the review board and the fact-check output.

07 · SOPs for AI

SOPs for AI — skills as the encoded method

Skills are files you can read; the role contract governs handoffs. The represented engagement uses transcript-mining (Researcher flags needs/frustrations; Synthesizer merges only distinct themes; the Critic gate rejects an insight that merely paraphrases one flagged line) and good-services-audit (score each heuristic with evidence; reject below-target scores lacking citations). Those method steps are source-verified through sd_method_run; the engagement data and conclusions are illustrative. The platform's deterministic core is evidence resolution, acceptance, review status, and fact-check. Cause analysis, stress models, and pricing remain human-team work.

Failure modes observed. (1) The "parts availability" cause claim was published early and was wrong — the dispatch attribution came from the analyst pass, not from the register; the critic gates the artifact, not the analysis. (2) A generated persona cited a nonexistent register row — dangling evidence refs are hard errors on write, so the artifact never left draft. (3) The naive 5% cap in an early contract draft — designer's analysis, corrected before launch.

10 · Lessons learned

Lessons learned & pitfalls

  1. The cause analysis is the analyst's — publish it behind an adversarial pass, not out of the register alone.
  2. Outcome contracts are artifact-design problems; this one survived legal because it was machine-checkable.
  3. Technicians are the product (book 20); co-designing the tooling beat any customer interview.
  4. The union boundary is design input; timing it early saved the pilot.
  5. Finance fights the risk pool; give them the model artifact, not a promise.
  6. Presenter packs from artifacts only — a chat-built deck once carried a stale MTTR; the export path (sd_export_report) renders only what is final.
