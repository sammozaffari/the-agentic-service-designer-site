The emotion row is the story: sentiment never lifts once the guest is inside. It holds at the counter, dips at payment when acknowledgment fails, and breaks hardest during the invisible production stage — the wait the guest cannot see and cannot trust. Handoff offers a partial recovery when the bag checks out. Every fail point is owned by the system, not the guest: F-01 through F-05 are the five places the design must intervene, from kiosk routing to pack-check to attribution rules.
