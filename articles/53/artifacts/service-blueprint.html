Line of visibility

The kiosk and the collection screen are the visible face; the POS, kitchen display, and packer chain are deliberately backstage. The foyer host's greeting is a frontstage act that must never be backstage: a guest must never have to ask who to talk to.

The huddle loop

The training ecosystem that makes the blueprint runnable: a 30-minute shift-huddle built around practice, competition, and recognition — the opposite of the passive e-learning that had never covered the channel. The knowledge check is the manager's evidence that the shift can run the channel alone.
