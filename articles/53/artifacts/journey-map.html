Where the human appears

The order workflow embeds three scripts at the exact moments the evidence says guests give up:

ScriptMomentParaphrase
GreetingThe door“Hi, I'm [name], your ordering host. Would you like faster, cashless ordering at the kiosk?”
AssistThe kioskHelp navigate, never judge; the counter is an equal path
DirectCash and specific piecesTo the counter queue, never abandoned at the machine

Read

Emotion is flat between the door and the collection point (2–3 of 5); the pains cluster exactly where no one is rostered to act. The fail points are the training and standards agenda — each one maps to a floor-role behaviour or a designed exception path.
