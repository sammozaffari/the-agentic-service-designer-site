Pilot design

A pilot in two markets, phased around a peak mid-year promotional trading period: market A first, then market B. Participants were the five largest franchisee groups plus independents, recruited through an expression-of-interest mechanism after demos at leadership forums, coach check-ins and roadshows — buy-in built before the test. Pilot metrics deliberately mirrored the business-case metrics: tickets in versus tickets out, completion-marking verification, a short agree-scale survey, and a running "what’s not working" feedback channel.

Headline results

0tickets raised against the new platform
1,000+training completions
100%of completed learning objects marked complete
100%of surveyed learners completed training without asking a manager

The guarantee the dual-system setup could not give: every completed learning object actually marked complete. The completion-marking verification was checked independently of the ticket count, so the two headlines stand on their own evidence.

Decision

The pilot results went to the franchisee education forum as a closed loop: the same numbers that built the business case now proved the design. The expedite request for the market-A rollout was approved on their strength — recorded internally as a decisive win — and the annual recertification cycle ran cleanly on top of the new dashboard. Evidence, not opinion, moved the rollout date.
