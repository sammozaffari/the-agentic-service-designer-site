Reading the map

The emotion line climbs because the system does the sequencing work. Each dip in the old journey — the content wall, the wait for a manager to say what’s next — has been replaced by a gate the platform owns. The one flat beat is the age gate: a deliberate moment of friction where eligibility is confirmed before restricted learning unlocks, interim until age data flows from the people platform. The fail-risk row shows the legacy failure each stage deleted.

The three compliance gates

GateRuleWhy it existsInterim?
Age confirmationLearner confirms minimum age before age-restricted learning unlocksAge data is not fed into any system; restricted content must still be blockedYes — a custom field plus self-confirmation until a data-level fix lands
Lock sequencing24-hour wait before day-2 induction modules; supervisor assessments not on the same day as web-based trainingPacing prevents skip-ahead and wrong-order completionNo — designed-in pacing
Prerequisite badgeOpen-procedures certification required before the supervisor-trained badgeCloses the loophole where the supervisor badge was earnable without open-procedures certificationNo — permanent

A fourth mechanism works alongside the gates: progressive disclosure as a compliance tool — only assigned focus areas appear, due and overdue indicators keep the path honest, mandatory-training markers flag what cannot be skipped, and the "show completed" toggle reduces clutter. UX craft applied to safety, not just usability.
