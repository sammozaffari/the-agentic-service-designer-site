About

Sam Mozaffari

I'm a service designer in Sydney. I design the whole arc of a service: research, synthesis, journey mapping, service blueprints, prototyping, pilots, measurement and governance. Along the way I built an agent team to carry the mechanical load, and it changed what the job is. My time goes to framing, judgment, ethics and the client relationship. The machines keep the receipts.

By day I design services at KFC South Pacific. This site exists because I think the discipline should be worked in public: the case studies, the courses, the ten-role harness, every artifact from research plan to pilot readout. All of it evidence-cited, and all of it free to read.

The practice

What I do

Full-spectrum service design

Research, synthesis, journey mapping, service blueprinting, ideation, prototyping, implementation, measurement and governance, all grounded in a 20-book library and shown end to end in every case study.

Agentic by default

Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, GitHub and the DeepSeek Harness are my working surface. A ten-role agent team does the mechanical load; SOPs-as-code make methods repeatable; CI makes artifacts verifiable.

Evidence before assertion

Every map, blueprint, persona and chart is built from an evidence register and checked by an adversarial critic. Numbers reconcile; nothing ships on vibes.

Client trust through fiction

The case studies are anonymized composites of real method and process, shared freely, with client privacy protected.

The method

How I build agents

Methods as code

Every technique in the practice is encoded before it runs: the prompt that drives it, the schema it must produce, the acceptance criteria it must meet and the review gate it must pass. That turns affinity synthesis, journey mapping or blueprinting from a craft habit into something repeatable, inspectable and auditable, runnable on any toolchain.

A team with contracts, not personas

The ten roles are not loose characters. The Orchestrator owns the engagement lifecycle and the phase gates. The Researcher owns the evidence register. The Critic sits on the write path, and nothing ships without its verdict. When the jobs are that explicit, work routes itself, reviews itself and explains itself.

The evidence register is the spine

Every interview, survey response and telemetry row lands in the register, and every map, persona and blueprint cites the rows it was built from. Numbers reconcile end to end. If a claim has no row behind it, it does not ship.

One practice, every surface

The same ten-role team ships as packs for Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex and the DeepSeek Harness, plus a standalone CLI and an MCP server. Same SOPs, same schemas, same gates, whatever surface you work on, and CI checks the artifacts as they are built.

The workflow

An engagement, end to end

The arc runs from research plan to pilot readout: two-hundred-interview research programmes, the synthesis wall, journey maps and service blueprints, ideation and prototyping with hypotheses rather than demos, and measurement and governance at the end. A team used to take a month to get through it; the agent team does it in days. Every phase closes at a gate. The critic checks the work, the evidence is reconciled, and only then does a human make the call. That is what makes the speed safe.

The ethos

Why this site is public

The practice is the proof

If the work can be run through agents, the working can be shown. Every case study carries the method behind it, and the method is encoded, cited and checkable. The archive is not a portfolio to admire; it is a process you can audit.

Free to read, free to take

None of this sits behind a paywall or a lead form. The methods, templates and packs are here to be read, copied and run. The discipline should not only exist inside client engagements.

Published is the new standard

Writing the work up in public changes how it is done. A claim that will be read has to carry its evidence; a method that will be run again has to be encoded; a practice that will be judged has to be honest about what is real and what is anonymized.

An archive of an emerging practice

This is a young discipline and the site is a record of it being built, not a finished statement. Case studies are anonymized composites, methods get revised and artifacts get better as the tools do. That is the point.
