About

Sam Mozaffari

For the last three and a half years, AI has been the point of my work: not an add-on, the point. I've folded agents into service design, into the software that delivers it, and into the research, prototyping and UI work around both. I still carry the taste from my design background: the framing, the judgment, the calls only a person should make. But my tools and working methods are fully AI now. The machines do the lifting; I do the deciding.

Which means the job titles stopped fitting. I'm not limited to UX design or service design anymore; I work horizontally across the full lifecycle of enterprise problem-solving: research, software building, prototype creation. Hire me for HR or P&C AI transformation, for engineering experience transformation, or for service design and UX. Either way, it's a team of one, plus as many agents as the work needs.

The practice

What I do

Applied AI, with a designer's taste

Three and a half years of deliberately integrating AI into service design, software development, research, prototyping and UI. The taste comes from the design side; the working method is agents. They are not in tension.

The full lifecycle, not just the map

Research, synthesis, mapping, ideation, prototyping, build, measurement and governance, with the working software and prototypes that come out the other end. The archive shows the whole arc, end to end.

Enterprise transformation

HR and P&C AI transformation, engineering experience transformation, service design and UX. Same approach everywhere: one designer, an agent team, and every claim carrying its evidence.

Evidence before assertion

Every map, blueprint, persona and chart is built from an evidence register and checked by an adversarial critic. Numbers reconcile; nothing ships on vibes.

The book

Agentic Service Design, bound

I wrote The Agentic Service Designer to put the whole practice between two covers: the theory (SDL, the Service Profit Chain, Good Services), the methods at machine scale (research ops, living journey maps, five- and six-lane blueprinting), and the platform that runs them. The Second Edition adds four dedicated platform chapters: the ASD MCP server, the plugin host packs and Claude Code native plugin, BUZZ multi-agent engagements, the DeepSeek Harness Studio, plus a fictional mastercase that runs one engagement across every surface. 25 chapters, ~80,000 words, real screenshots, honest evidence boundaries. It is a living document: as the platform changes, the book changes with it.

Inside the Second Edition:

  • Part I–IV: the full discipline, from foundations to research, mapping, delivery and operations.
  • Part V (new): MCP server · plugins & host packs · BUZZ · DSH Studio, in depth with captures.
  • Part VI: SOPs-as-code, Good Services, the Meridian Bay Ferries mastercase, the future.
  • Appendices: 80-method catalog, 30-tool CLI/MCP specification, 20-book cross-reference.
