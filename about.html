About

Sam Mozaffari

I'm a service designer, and I run my practice through AI agents. Coding two hundred interviews, rebuilding the synthesis wall, drafting the blueprints — that used to take a team a month. An agent team does it in days now, and the work is more documented, not less: every claim carries its evidence, and a critic reviews it at every gate before anything ships. The framing, the judgment, the ethics and the client's trust stay with me. The machines keep the receipts.

By day I design services at KFC South Pacific. This site is the working archive of all of it — 36 published case studies, a ten-role agent harness, and every artifact from research plan to pilot readout. Everything is evidence-cited, and all of it is free to read.

The practice

What I do

Full-spectrum service design

Research, synthesis, journey mapping, service blueprinting, ideation, prototyping, implementation, measurement and governance — the complete arc, grounded in a 20-book library, shown end to end in every case study.

Agentic by default

Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, GitHub and the DeepSeek Harness are my working surface. A ten-role agent team does the mechanical load; SOPs-as-code make methods repeatable; CI makes artifacts verifiable.

Evidence before assertion

Every map, blueprint, persona and chart is built from an evidence register and checked by an adversarial critic. Numbers reconcile; nothing ships on vibes.

Client trust through fiction

The case studies are anonymized composites — real method and process, shared freely, client privacy protected.
