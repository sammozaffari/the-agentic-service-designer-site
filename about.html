About

Sam Mozaffari

I'm a service designer who runs the discipline through AI agents. What used to take a team and a month — coding two hundred interviews, rebuilding the synthesis wall, drafting the blueprints — now runs through an agent team in days, with every claim carrying its evidence and a critic on every gate. The designer keeps framing, judgment, ethics and client trust. The machines keep the receipts.

By day I design services at KFC South Pacific. This site is the practice behind the practice: 36 published case studies, a ten-role agent harness, and every artifact from research plan to pilot readout — AI-first, evidence-cited, and all of it free to read.

The practice

What I do

Full-spectrum service design

Research, synthesis, journey mapping, service blueprinting, ideation, prototyping, implementation, measurement and governance — the complete arc, grounded in a 20-book library, shown end to end in every case study.

Agentic by default

Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, GitHub and the DeepSeek Harness are my working surface. A ten-role agent team does the mechanical load; SOPs-as-code make methods repeatable; CI makes artifacts verifiable.

Evidence before assertion

Every map, blueprint, persona and chart is built from an evidence register and checked by an adversarial critic. Numbers reconcile; nothing ships on vibes.

Client trust through fiction

The case studies are anonymized composites — real method and process, shared freely, client privacy protected.
