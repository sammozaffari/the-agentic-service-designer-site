Sam Mozaffari

I am a service designer who runs the discipline through AI agents. Thirty anonymized case studies — built with Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex and GitHub as the primary tools, and grounded in a 20-book service design library — show the full spectrum of the craft: research, synthesis, mapping, ideation, prototyping, implementation, measurement and governance.

What I do

A full-spectrum service designer who compresses weeks of research-and-mapping into agent-driven iterations — without giving up the judgment that makes design decisions defensible.

Design at scale

200-interview research programs, 1.8M-event telemetry, 96k–2.4M-unit service systems — the series shows service design that holds up against real operational data.

AI as the working surface

Not “we used AI” — a repeatable practice: agent teams, SOPs-as-code, CI gates, adversarial review. Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex and GitHub are the tools of record in every case study.

Evidence before assertion

Every map, blueprint and chart in the series is built from evidence and checked by an adversarial reviewer. Numbers reconcile; nothing ships on vibes.

Client trust through fiction

The case studies are anonymized composites — real method and process, shared freely, client privacy protected.

The agentic service designer — my approach

Service design methods produce reliable results only when executed with discipline, and discipline is exactly what a standardised, versioned, reviewable procedure gives an AI agent. I encode each method as an SOP-with-code — prompt, output schema, acceptance criteria, review gate — then run the engagement through a small team of specialised agents: researcher, synthesizer, mapper, ideator, presenter, and a critic that attacks every artefact before a human signs it off.

The result is a practice that is faster, more consistent, and fully traceable — every card cites its evidence, every chart reconciles, every blueprint is kept honest by CI.
