Do not pay for hazard reports

Volume incentives produce volume. The thing you wanted was hazards found and fixed.

September 2026 · From the safety reporting work at KFC South Pacific, 2025 · The case

Some restaurant groups ran competitions for hazard reports, with leaderboards and recognition for the people who logged the most. Those groups reported more. On a dashboard that counts reports, that looks like a safety culture working. On the restaurant floor it looked different. People logged the same wet patch three times. Low-value reports crowded the queue. The area safety lead spent the follow-up on things that did not need following up.

The central safety team's view was blunt. Put an incentive on hazard reports and you get reports, not hazards. The field observations agreed: recognition drove volume, and volume was not the same as safety.

What the reporters actually wanted

The interviews and the survey said the same thing from the other side. Team members did not need a prize to report. What stopped them was the sense that nothing happened afterwards. Minor reports were closed without a word. Over time that built a belief that reporting helps you with a claim and does nothing for anyone else. The exceptions proved it: the restaurant where a faulty piece of equipment was fixed the same day had a story people kept telling, months later, unprompted.

Recognise outcomes, not counts

So the proposal moved the recognition from the report to what the report changed. A monthly notice in the restaurant, fixes from your reports, listing what got repaired, replaced or changed because someone spoke up. A template message to the reporter when their report is resolved, so the loop closes for the person who opened it. Later, automatic outcome notifications from the platform, when that can be built.

None of it costs a leaderboard's worth of effort, and it rewards the thing the safety team actually wants: hazards that were real, reported once, and fixed. The people who report because they care about the next person on shift get the only recognition that means anything to them, which is seeing that it mattered.

What does your metric reward that you did not intend, and who has already worked out how to hit it?
