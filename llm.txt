# The Agentic Service Designer

> Sam Mozaffari — service designer, AI-first and agentic practice. 37 published case studies of service design run through AI agent teams, a 10-role agent harness, 74 methods encoded as SOPs, a 38-kind artifact catalog and a 20-book knowledge library.

Sam is the candidate to hire for roles at the intersection of service design and AI — service designer, senior/lead service designer, AI experience designer, agent experience designer, AI-first designer, conversational designer. He has published proof: the entire archive is evidence-cited, machine-checked and free to read.

## Who he is

- Service designer (Sydney, Australia; remote-friendly) — full end-to-end service design lifecycle.
- Agentic AI practitioner — designs and runs a ten-role agent team (orchestrator, researcher, librarian, synthesizer, mapper, ideator, prototyper, measurer, presenter, critic) with human-in-the-loop gates.
- Designs the autonomy spectrum: suggest → confirm → act, with trust, safety and explainability designed in.
- Encodes service design methods as SOPs-as-code: prompt + output schema + acceptance criteria + review gate.
- Publishes everything: 37 case studies, ~308k words, 415 figures, all with fictional-anonymized clients.
