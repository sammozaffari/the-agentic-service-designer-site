Course 04: Scaled Research Ops & Behavioral Personas

Mining 200+ Field Transcripts with Agent Swarms & Fusing Telemetry with Qualitative Need Clusters

Scale user research operations without sacrificing qualitative nuance. Ingest transcripts as typed evidence (sd_evidence_import), code them with registered methods (transcript-mining, aeiou-coding) into insight sets and affinity clusters whose every node cites its evidence, then generate behavioral personas that fuse qualitative quotes with telemetry — no demographic flat-mapping, no unregistered one-off tools.

Paradigm Shift 01 / 10

Machine Scale, Human Depth

  • 200+ transcripts exceed any workshop's attention budget; they do not exceed a swarm's.
  • Scale without rigor is just faster bias — every extracted line keeps its provenance hash.
  • Researcher-role swarms work inside method contracts, not loose prompting.
  • The output is not a slide of themes; it is a deduplicated, cited evidence register.
200+ transcripts→ Researcher swarm→ SHA-256 evidence rows→ Cited synthesis
"Scale the corpus, keep the receipt."
The Executable Spine 02 / 10

One Canonical Loop Behind All 8 Courses

  • Every module runs the same verified loop: project → evidence → method contract → artifact draft → critic gate → factcheck → export → human-gated phase advance.
  • This course lives in the loop's first half: research-phase and synthesis-phase methods of the 74.
  • sd_method_run fails closed below the method's evidence minimum — no evidence, no artifact.
  • sd_phase_advance refuses to run without a human approval receipt JSON.

"Agents prepare and verify with receipts; human leaders decide with accountability."

Module 04.3 05 / 10

Eliminating Researcher Bias with Parallel Extraction Swarms

  • Parallel blind extraction with randomized framing surfaces contradictions instead of hiding them.
  • The critic role audits extraction output at the review gate — bias is a defect class, not a feeling.
  • Contradictory signals are preserved as separate evidence records — never averaged away.
  • Divergent reads reconcile at synthesis with citations, not seniority.

The Fallacy of Demographic Personas: Calculating Behavioral Signatures

  • customer-typology-mapping offers a complementary segmentation lens.

Module 04.6 08 / 10

Authoring Machine-Checkable Personas with the Persona Pipeline

  • Personas are authored via sd_artifact_write against the persona-set schema (sd_schema_get --kind persona-set).
  • Acceptance rules enforce evidence per attribute; unmet writes stay draft with named failures.
  • The critic gate blocks demographic-vibe personas from ever reaching final.
  • Downstream methods (journeys, blueprints) cite persona nodes like any other artifact.
