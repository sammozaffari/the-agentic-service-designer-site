Service Architecture 01 / 02

Why Blueprints Are Service Architecture

  • Service blueprints translate customer experience into operational specifications.
  • Demarcate the 5 canonical lanes: Physical Evidence, Customer Actions, Frontstage, Backstage, Support.
  • Establish clear lines of Visibility, Interaction, and Internal Penetration.
Customer Actions --- Visibility --- Frontstage Staff --- Penetration --- Backstage & Support
"If a service experience is not blueprint-grounded, it is merely wishful thinking."
AI Extension 02 / 02

The Sixth Lane: Autonomous Agent Swarms

  • Traditional 5-lane blueprints fail when autonomous AI agents act as active decision-makers.
  • Lane 6 models agent swarms, LLM prompts, tool invocations, and probabilistic confidence thresholds.
  • Explicit fallback escalation routes to Frontstage Human staff when confidence drops.
Customer Input ➔ Lane 6: Agent Decision (Conf ≥ 0.90) ➔ Auto-Execute or Escalate to Human
"Modeling probabilistic AI decisions in blueprints prevents catastrophic silent failovers."
