Module 06.1 03 / 11

Why Blueprints Are the Architecture of Service Delivery

  • Blueprints specify what must be true operationally for an experience to exist.
  • They bind customer actions to the staff actions, systems, and processes that deliver them.
  • service-blueprinting and evidence-gated-blueprint produce service-blueprint artifacts.
  • An unblueprinted service cannot be audited, staffed, or automated responsibly.

Module 06.5 07 / 11

Confidence Thresholds, Error Cascades & Human-in-the-Loop Escalation Paths

  • Every agent decision carries a confidence score and an explicit threshold.
  • Above threshold: auto-execute inside the blueprint. Below: escalate to a frontstage human with full context.
  • Error cascades are designed out with deterministic fallback loops — no silent failovers.
  • Escalation is a first-class blueprint edge, not an operational surprise.
Agent decision→confidence ≥ threshold?
AboveAuto-execute within the blueprint, logged
BelowEscalate to frontstage human with full context
"Confidence below threshold is not a failure; silence about it is."

Automated Blueprint Diagnostics, Validation & Bottleneck Audits
