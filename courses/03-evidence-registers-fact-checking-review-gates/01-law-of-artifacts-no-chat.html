📖 1. Theoretical Grounding & Literature Synthesis

Academic & Practice Foundations

In traditional service design, methods have often been treated as informal, transient workshop exercises. However, rigorous academic literature—from Vargo & Lusch (Service-Dominant Logic), Christian Grönroos (Service Quality Management), Mary Jo Bitner & Lynn Shostack (Service Blueprinting), to Lou Downe (Good Services) and Jim Kalbach (Mapping Experiences)—has long demanded that services be treated as complex, adaptive, socio-technical systems.

When we augment service design with autonomous AI agents, we do not invent a new discipline from scratch; rather, we provide the computational infrastructure necessary to execute these canonical theories at scale.

Core Methodological Invariants

  • Referential Integrity: Every claim made in downstream deliverables must resolve to an immutable cryptographic SHA-256 hash in .asd/evidence.db.
  • Typing Over Chat Memory: No critical design handoffs occur in conversational context windows. All state is serialized to structured JSON on disk.
  • Adversarial Falsification: The Critic agent sits on the write path and actively attempts to disprove design assertions before client presentation.
