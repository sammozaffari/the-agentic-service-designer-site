Course 07: Cross-Channel Orchestration, Prototyping & SOPs-as-Code

Narrative Arcs, Omnichannel Transitions, Morphological Ideation, Wizard-of-Oz Testing, and Versioned SOPs

Connect high-level strategy to frontline operational execution. Orchestrate fragmented channels into seamless narratives with story maps, storyboards, and registered ecosystem maps; execute machine-speed traceable ideation (hmw-set → concept-set under NUF triple-lens filtering encoded as critic rows); prototype with Wizard-of-Oz and concierge plans; and replace stagnant SOP binders with service-standard-sets and change-plans rolled out through real orchestration dispatch.

Paradigm Shift 01 / 10

Services Are Choreographed, Not Documented

  • Customers experience one service across many channels; organizations ship many departments.
  • Orchestration is designed choreography: channel roles, transition protocols, context carryover — explicit and executable.
  • SOPs stop being binders and become tested, versioned, schema-validated code.
  • Prototypes climb a gated ladder from tabletop to Wizard-of-Oz before engineering spends a dollar.
Channel silos→ Orchestration canvas→ Typed handoffs→ One seamless experience
"The customer experiences one service; orchestration makes the organization act like it."
The Executable Spine 02 / 10

One Canonical Loop Behind All 8 Courses

  • Every module runs the same verified loop: project → evidence → method contract → artifact draft → critic gate → factcheck → export → human-gated phase advance.
  • This course runs the ideation and prototyping phases of the method catalog.
  • sd_method_run fails closed below the method's evidence minimum — no evidence, no artifact.
  • sd_phase_advance refuses to run without a human approval receipt JSON.
sd_project_create→ sd_evidence_add→ sd_method_run→ sd_artifact_write→ sd_review→ sd_factcheck→ sd_export_report→ sd_phase_advance
"Agents prepare and verify with receipts; human leaders decide with accountability."
Module 07.1 03 / 10

Omnichannel Fragmentation & The Cross-Channel Orchestration Canvas

  • orchestration-canvas defines channel roles, transition protocols, and context carryover.
  • sd_orchestration_plan builds the run plan; sd_orchestration_status tracks it live.
  • sd_orchestration_dispatch and sd_orchestration_handoff move typed artifacts between roles — never chat memory.
  • Context carryover is designed: the customer never repeats themselves at a channel boundary.
orchestration_plan→ orchestration_dispatch→ orchestration_handoff→ orchestration_status
"Orchestration moves artifacts, not conversations."
Module 07.2 04 / 10

Service Story Mapping: Narrative Arcs, Climax Points & Experience Staging

  • Pine & Gilmore: experiences are staged; story-mapping borrows narrative structure for service moments.
  • story-mapping produces story-map artifacts with deliberate arcs and climax points.
  • storyboard-authoring stages each moment as a storyboard ready for walkthrough testing.
  • Flat services are a design choice too — made consciously, with the arc on record.
Setup→ Rising action→ Climax moment→ Resolution & recall
"Stage the moment or the moment stages itself."
Module 07.3 05 / 10

Machine-Speed Ideation: Insight → HMW → Morphological Matrix → NUF Filter

  • Strict lineage: insight-set → hmw-formulation (hmw-set) → morphological-concept-matrix (concept-set).
  • nuf-triple-lens-filter scores concepts on Novelty, Usefulness, Feasibility — divergence without slop.
  • Every HMW cites its insight; every concept cites its HMW — ideation with provenance.
  • Machine speed is safe precisely because the lineage is machine-checked.
insight-set→ hmw-set→ concept-set (matrix)→ NUF-scored shortlist
"Divergence with lineage is ideation; divergence without it is slop."
Module 07.4 06 / 10

Rapid Service Prototyping: Tabletop Walkthroughs to Wizard-of-Oz Agentic Backends

  • prototype-ladder-gating climbs tabletop → desktop-walkthrough → concierge-pilot with gates between rungs.
  • wizard-of-oz tests full service interactions with agent backends before engineering.
  • service-roleplay and bodystorming keep the body in the loop early and cheap.
  • Each rung produces a typed artifact (prototype-plan, walkthrough-script, test-report) with its own gate.
Tabletop→ Desktop walkthrough→ Wizard-of-Oz→ Concierge pilot
"Test the service before you build it; the ladder has gates for a reason."
Module 07.5 07 / 10

The Failure of the SOP Binder: Engineering SOPs-as-Code

  • service-standard-authoring produces service-standard-set artifacts: SOPs as schema-validated code.
  • Author, test, version, audit — the binder is dead; the standard is executable.
  • raci-definition assigns accountable roles to every standard step.
  • Standards drift is detected by the same factcheck loop as every other artifact.
Author (schema)→ Test (walkthrough)→ Version (artifact store)→ Audit (factcheck)
"The binder is dead; the standard is executable."
Module 07.6 08 / 10

Co-Designing Frontline Co-Pilots & Context Handover Without Screen Tyranny

  • frontline-enablement designs co-pilots that give staff superhuman memory without screen tyranny.
  • Context handover rides sd_orchestration_handoff: typed state, not a retold story.
  • Co-pilot suggestions are proposals; the frontline human keeps sovereign authority.
  • Cognitive load is a design constraint with the same status as any acceptance rule.
