Course 07: Cross-Channel Orchestration, Prototyping & SOPs-as-Code

Narrative Arcs, Omnichannel Transitions, Morphological Ideation, Wizard-of-Oz Testing, and Versioned SOPs

Connect high-level strategy to frontline operational execution. Orchestrate fragmented channels into seamless narratives using the Orchestration Canvas and Story Mapping. Execute machine-speed ideation using morphological matrices and the NUF filter. Build live Wizard-of-Oz prototypes with agentic backends. Replace stagnant SOP binders with executable SOPs-as-Code.
