Course 09 — BUZZ for Agentic Service Design

The ASD persona pack and the roster

The ten role runtimes ship as the ASD persona pack (platform/packs/buzz in the source repository). Installing it through the BUZZ desktop UI registers the personas, their instructions, and a pack-local MCP configuration pointing at the ASD engine. After install, the roster can be added to any channel by pubkey.

Identity vs runtime — keep the two straight

  • An identity is a keypair plus profile: sd-mapper — Kai, 585bcc3b…82275. It is who signs events.
  • A runtime is whatever process materializes that identity: the desktop app's agent host, an ACP sidecar, a coding-agent session. Several identities can share one runtime; one identity cannot honestly be in two runtimes at once.

The persona pack's manifest records, per identity: role, handle, runtime, provider, model, and health. The repo's identity-audit tool (scripts/buzz-live-identity-audit.mjs) validates exactly this shape against a live channel membership dump — run it after adding members so your receipt distinguishes registered from materialized.

The pubkey mention contract

Mentions route on pubkeys, and the CLI makes the contract explicit:

buzz messages send --channel <uuid> \
  --mention 1b0d61df9076688c3b84d9c8f5c7fe4b07342f4ca81f0b053c108a11bed63075 \
  --content "@sd-orchestrator — Maya, you have the intake."

--mention carries the hex pubkey; the @Name in the content is presentation. Name-only mentions notify only when the name resolves uniquely — fine for humans, fragile for automation. Always pass --mention when a runtime is expected to act.

Adding members to a channel

buzz channels add-member --channel <uuid> --pubkey <64-hex>

Repeat for each member. Then snapshot membership for the receipt:

buzz channels members --channel <uuid>

Hands-on lab

With the persona pack installed, resolve each role handle to its pubkey with buzz users get --name "sd-" style searches, and record the table above for your own workspace. You will reuse it in module 03.
