Course 08: Service Quality, Good Services Audits & Continuous Operations

Grönroos Perceived Quality, Lou Downe's 15 Principles Machine Audit, Service Recovery SLAs & Continuous Blueprints

The capstone course for enterprise service leaders. Implement Grönroos Perceived Quality, Parasuraman's 5 GAPs, and 3-tier KPI trees linking operational telemetry to financial ROI. Automate Lou Downe's 15 Principles of Good Services with good-services-audit-15. Engineer automated recovery playbooks to capitalize on the Service Recovery Paradox. Transition to Continuous Service Architecture.
