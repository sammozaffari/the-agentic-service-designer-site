Module 01.1: The Death of Workshop Theater: Moving to an Executable Service OS

Course: Agentic Service Design Fundamentals · Level: Foundation · Duration: 50 min · Format: Theory, Architecture, Verified CLI Run & Hands-on Lab

Learning Objectives

By completing this module, you will be able to:

  1. Diagnose workshop theater — name the exact mechanisms (hierarchy distortion, unprovenanced synthesis, artifact decay) that make sticky-note deliverables rot, citing This is Service Design Doing and Kumar's 101 Design Methods.
  2. Explain evidence-gated practice — how a content-addressed evidence store, typed artifact contracts, and an adversarial critic gate replace the research wall without losing its collaborative virtues.
  3. Run the canonical loop — project-create → evidence-add → method-run → artifact-write → review → factcheck → export-report → phase-advance against the real @asd runtime in a disposable workspace.
  4. Read a method contract — owner, step roles, acceptance rules, human checkpoints — before writing a single artifact node.

1. Theoretical Grounding: Why Workshops Became Theater

1.1 What the canon actually says about workshops

Stickdorn, Lawrence, Hormess and Schneider devote a full chapter of This is Service Design Doing (2018, Ch. 8, Facilitating Workshops — summarized in books/01-this-is-service-design-doing/08-facilitating-workshops.md) to a warning most teams skip: a workshop is itself a service, and it must be designed — pre-workshop onboarding, in-session cognitive rhythm, and post-workshop offboarding with "rapid photographic documentation, synthesized decision memos, and clear ownership tracking." The same chapter gives the facilitator explicit countermeasures for hierarchy distortion: silent generation before verbal sharing because "the HiPPO (Highest Paid Person's Opinion)" otherwise anchors the room; parking lots for solution-jumpers; round-robin turns for passive observers.

Notice what those countermeasures admit: the default physics of a workshop corrupt the data. Verbal-first brainstorming is dominated by rank. Synthesis on a physical wall is anonymous, unversioned, and gone when the room is released. The chapter's own definition of success is a single mandatory output artifact — "if no tangible artifact is needed, cancel the workshop" — yet the artifact it produces (a photographed wall) has no provenance: you cannot ask a sticky note which participant said it, in what context, or whether two notes contradict.

Chapter 4 of the same book (Research Methods, books/01-this-is-service-design-doing/04-research-methods.md) sets the bar the wall fails to clear:

  • Say vs. Do — reported attitudes are not observed behavior; valid research triangulates direct observation, contextual inquiry, and secondary quantitative data.
  • The Research Wall — the physical synthesis surface where "verbatim quotes, photos, video stills, artifact scans, and field notes" are externalized as the team's shared working memory. The function is correct: externalized, shared, inspectable evidence. The medium is the problem: paper cannot enforce that an insight statement cites its quotes.
  • Inductive synthesis — data → themes → insights, bottom-up, never forced into predetermined frameworks. Any tooling that hard-codes conclusions before evidence violates this.

1.2 Kumar: innovation is a process, not an event

Vijay Kumar's 101 Design Methods (2012, Ch. 1, books/17-101-design-methods/01-innovation-as-a-process.md) states the second half of the indictment: "innovation is not an unpredictable accident of individual genius or unguided brainstorming; it is a disciplined, teachable, and repeatable management process." His four principles — build around experiences, think in systems, cultivate an innovation culture, adopt a disciplined innovation process — reframe the one-off workshop as a category error. The Seven Modes (Sense Intent → Know Context → Know People → Frame Insights → Explore Concepts → Frame Solutions → Realize Offerings) are explicitly non-linear with looping: Mode 5 loops back to Mode 4 when concept gaps appear. A workshop series that ends in a static PDF cannot loop; a process whose state lives in files can.

1.3 The diagnosis, precisely

Workshop theater is what happens when the rituals of service design (sticky notes, dot voting, journey posters) are performed without the invariants the literature demands:

Invariant (from the literature) Workshop theater violation
Triangulation of say/do/observe (TISDD Ch. 4) One round of interviews, summarized from memory
HiPPO countermeasures (TISDD Ch. 8) Verbal brainstorms anchored by the senior voice
Verbatim provenance on the research wall Photographed wall; quotes detached from sources
Disciplined, repeatable process (Kumar Ch. 1) Deliverable is a deck; method steps unreproducible
Non-linear looping between modes Looping costs a new workshop; so nobody loops

2. Agentic Topology: The Research Wall, Rebuilt as Infrastructure

The Agentic Service Design runtime keeps the function of the research wall — externalized, shared, inspectable evidence — and replaces the medium with a per-project JSON store under ASD_WORKSPACE (default ~/.asd/projects). Every piece of evidence is content-addressed: its id is the SHA-256 of [source, kind, body, origin], so a quote cannot be silently edited after something cites it.

The roles in this module's slice of the 10-role team (module 01.3 covers the full topology):

Role Responsibility in this module Tools it actually calls
researcher Captures verbatim quotes, telemetry, and policy documents as typed evidence records (kind quote/telemetry/document — nothing else is accepted). sd_evidence_add, sd_evidence_import, sd_evidence_query
mapper Owns the journey-from-transcripts method contract; reads the contract before writing a journey-map draft. sd_method_run, sd_artifact_write
critic The review gate. Rejects any stage whose emotion or pain point is not supported by a quoted line. sd_review, sd_factcheck
orchestrator Records the human-gated phase decision once artifacts are final. sd_phase_advance

The invariant contract

Handoffs between roles are typed filesystem artifacts, never conversational memory. A role that cannot cite an evidence id has nothing to say.

3. Verified Execution: The Canonical Loop (run today, 2026-08-22)

Everything below was executed against the built runtime (suite/packages/runtime/lib/cli.mjs) in a disposable workspace. Ids are real output from that run; your run will mint different hashes because they are content- and time-derived.

export ASD_WORKSPACE=/tmp/asd-lab   # per-project JSON store
CLI="node suite/packages/runtime/lib/cli.mjs"

Step 1 — create the project (starts in phase discover):

$CLI project-create --name "Fernwood Repairs Sprint" --client "Fernwood Council" \
  --sector government --service "housing repairs reporting"

{"project":{"projectId":"fernwood-repairs-sprint-f0d60fa1","phase":"discover", ...}}

Step 2 — capture evidence (researcher role). Note the fail-closed taxonomy: --kind observation is rejected with "arguments.kind" must be one of ["quote","telemetry","document"]. The tool forces you to classify the nature of the source before storing it — TISDD's triangulation made structural.

$CLI evidence-add --projectId fernwood-repairs-sprint-f0d60fa1 --source int-01 --kind quote \
  --body "I reported the damp patch three times on the portal and nobody ever told me what happened next."

{"evidence":{"id":"fd46196ccb48342bb8d7bb237e7522b50828f5070e38d8dfd8668fd8790eaeab",
 "source":"int-01","kind":"quote","capturedAt":"2026-08-22T11:35:17.357Z"}}

Step 3 — read the method contract before writing anything:

$CLI method-run --projectId fernwood-repairs-sprint-f0d60fa1 --methodId journey-from-transcripts

{"method":{"id":"journey-from-transcripts","phase":"synthesis","owner":"mapper","reviewGate":"critic"},
 "steps":[
   {"role":"researcher","instruction":"Extract every distinct customer action, channel and breakdown from the transcripts, keeping the verbatim quote for each."},
   {"role":"mapper","instruction":"Order the actions into stages. Give each stage an emotion drawn from the quotes, never inferred."},
   {"role":"critic","instruction":"Reject any stage whose emotion or pain point is not supported by a quoted line.","gate":true}],
 "acceptance":[{"rule":"min-nodes","min":3},{"rule":"min-evidence-per-node","min":1},
   {"rule":"field-non-empty","field":"emotion"},{"rule":"field-non-empty","field":"touchpoint"}],
 "inputs":[{"kind":"evidence-set","min":3}],
 "outputKind":"journey-map","availableEvidence":4}

The contract fails closed: with only 2 evidence records in the project, the same command exits 1 with method "journey-from-transcripts" requires at least 3 evidence records; project has 2. Capture evidence with sd_evidence_add first. You cannot start mapping from an empty wall — Kumar's discipline enforced by exit code.

Step 4 — write the draft artifact (mapper role). A node that cites an unregistered hash is refused at write time:

$CLI artifact-write --projectId fernwood-repairs-sprint-f0d60fa1 --methodId journey-from-transcripts \
  --title "Tenant repairs journey" \
  --nodes '[{"id":"n1","label":"Report damp","evidence":[{"evidenceId":"deadbeef..."}],"fields":{"emotion":"x","touchpoint":"portal"}}]'
# → asd artifact-write: node "n1" cites evidence "deadbeef..." which is not in the project evidence store  (exit 1)

A valid write returns {"accepted":true,"status":"draft","artifact":{"id":"2baab028b1fb4cc997d5f8d977a049f5","kind":"journey-map", ...}} — module 01.6 walks this full artifact end to end.

Step 5 — critic gate, factcheck, export, human-gated advance — covered with real receipts in modules 01.4 and 01.6.

TypeScript contract (@asd/core)

// suite/packages/core/src/types.ts — the artifact every method produces
export type Artifact = {
  readonly id: string
  readonly kind: ArtifactKind          // 32 registered kinds; "journey-map" here
  readonly projectId: string
  readonly title: string
  readonly schemaVersion: number       // 1
  readonly methodId?: string           // "journey-from-transcripts"
  readonly nodes: readonly ArtifactNode[]  // { id, label, evidence: EvidenceRef[], fields }
  readonly edges?: readonly ArtifactEdge[] // { id, from, to, relation, fields } — all required
  readonly status: 'draft' | 'final'   // final = schema ✓ evidence ✓ acceptance ✓ review pass
  readonly check?: ArtifactCheck       // retained acceptance failures while draft
  readonly review?: ArtifactReview     // { verdict: 'pass' | 'veto', logArtifactId }
}

Worked Example: Six Transcript Lines → Three Typed Records → One Citable Insight Register

The fixture. Six lines from a 25-minute discovery session (ws-01) at Callorna Clinic, on appointment rescheduling:

P1: I tried to move my physio appointment online and the form made me start over three times.
P1: In the end I just called, and I was on hold for forty minutes.
R1: Half my morning is rebooking people who gave up on the portal.
P2: I got a text saying my appointment changed but it didn't say to what.
R1: The system sends the confirmation before we finish the slot swap, so the message is often wrong.
P2: I turned up on the wrong day because I trusted the text.

Step 1 — classify before you capture. Every line is reported speech, so kind is quote — never observation (rejected by the taxonomy) and never paraphrase. Lines P1's two statements are one episode, so they travel as one record body. Three records, three sources (ws-01-p1, ws-01-r1, ws-01-p2).

Step 2 — capture. sd_evidence_add per record mints content-addressed ids (real output, disposable run 2026-08-23):

{"id":"8b603633b761c25af271d270b1dc4c96e04e9d2d23d5115e24d530ed6d99e1a2","source":"ws-01-p2","kind":"quote"}
{"id":"bad03326aca8e84aafed186643bcd6de2ce4c0f189868f47a9daa5e75725b579","source":"ws-01-r1","kind":"quote"}
{"id":"f70abec029d50b58237d678e984cb2d0fca51139407f6625bea306125e99d0ad","source":"ws-01-p1","kind":"quote"}

Step 3 — synthesize into the insight register. The register is artifact kind insight-set (produced by insight-statement-formulation; node field insight required, edges forbidden — insights cite evidence, not each other). sd_artifact_write with two nodes:

{"accepted":true,"status":"draft",
 "artifact":{"id":"6943a57bbf39402ca1f8d3d0c0ff8b2b","kind":"insight-set",
  "methodId":"insight-statement-formulation","status":"draft"},
 "nodes":[
  {"id":"i1","label":"Confirmation-before-completion defect",
   "evidence":[{"evidenceId":"bad03326…b579"},{"evidenceId":"8b603633…e1a2"}],
   "fields":{"insight":"The flow fires the confirmation SMS before the slot swap completes, so patients act on wrong information.","confidence":"high","theme":"broken-notification","opportunity":"Gate the SMS on transaction completion."}},
  {"id":"i2","label":"Self-service abandonment into phone queue",
   "evidence":[{"evidenceId":"f70abec0…90ad"}],
   "fields":{"insight":"Form restarts push patients off the portal into a 40-minute phone queue, converting a digital task into failure demand.","confidence":"medium","theme":"channel-collapse","opportunity":"Persist form state across errors."}}]}

Interpretation. The provenance chain is now unbroken and auditable in both directions: insight i1 cites bad03326… and 8b603633…, which resolve to R1's and P2's exact words, which trace to session ws-01. Nobody can later "improve" the quotes — editing the body changes the SHA-256 and dangles every citation. Confidence is honestly asymmetric: i1 is corroborated by two speakers on opposite sides of the counter (high); i2 rests on one patient (medium) and is flagged for triangulation with portal telemetry before it can anchor an investment case. A photographed sticky-note wall cannot express any of this — who said it, how sure we are, or what would make us surer.

5. Hands-On Lab

Work in a disposable workspace — nothing here touches your real projects.

export ASD_WORKSPACE=/tmp/asd-lab-01
CLI="node suite/packages/runtime/lib/cli.mjs"

# 1. Create a project; note phase starts at "discover"
$CLI project-create --name "Workshop Detox" --client "Your org" --sector government --service "permit applications"

# 2. Capture three evidence records of DIFFERENT kinds (triangulation)
$CLI evidence-add --projectId <pid> --source int-01 --kind quote --body "<a real verbatim sentence>"
$CLI evidence-add --projectId <pid> --source tel-01 --kind telemetry --body "<a metric with a number in it>"
$CLI evidence-add --projectId <pid> --source doc-01 --kind document --body "<a policy excerpt>"

# 3. Fail-closed demo: observation is not a kind (exit 1 expected)
$CLI evidence-add --projectId <pid> --source int-02 --kind observation --body "Users seemed unhappy." ; echo "exit=$?"

# 4. Read the method contract; find owner, gate, acceptance, inputs.min
$CLI method-run --projectId <pid> --methodId journey-from-transcripts

# 5. Fail-closed demo: write a node citing a fake hash (exit 1 expected)
$CLI artifact-write --projectId <pid> --methodId journey-from-transcripts --title "t" \
  --nodes '[{"id":"n1","label":"x","evidence":[{"evidenceId":"'$(printf 'a%.0s' {1..64})'"}],"fields":{"emotion":"e","touchpoint":"t"}}]' ; echo "exit=$?"

Verification checklist

  • Project created with phase: "discover" and a slug-suffixed projectId.
  • All three evidence kinds accepted; observation rejected with exit 1.
  • Method contract shows owner: "mapper", reviewGate: "critic", inputs[0].min: 3, and availableEvidence equal to your record count.
  • Dangling-hash write rejected with cites evidence ... which is not in the project evidence store.
  • You can explain, in one sentence each, which workshop-theater failure each fail-closed behavior prevents.

Key Takeaways

  • Workshop theater is a provenance failure, not a facilitation failure. The literature (TISDD Ch. 4/8, Kumar Ch. 1) already demanded verbatim evidence, triangulation, and repeatable process; rooms full of sticky notes simply cannot enforce them.
  • The research wall's function survives; its medium is replaced. A SHA-256 content-addressed evidence store keeps everything the wall was for — shared, externalized, inspectable — and adds immutability and citability.
  • Method contracts fail closed. Insufficient evidence, dangling citations, and unclassified sources are exit-1 errors, not meeting action items.
  • The loop is the deliverable. evidence → contract → draft → critic gate → final → factcheck → export → human-gated advance is reproducible by anyone with the repo; a workshop never is.
