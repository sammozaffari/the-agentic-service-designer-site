Paradigm Shift 01 / 10

From Sticky Notes to an Executable Service OS

  • Workshop theater produces static artifacts that decay the day the engagement ends — nothing is queryable, nothing re-validates.
  • Research → mapping → engineering handoffs lose fidelity at every step because context travels as conversation.
  • The Agentic Service Design OS treats service design as a file-backed, machine-checked system of record.
  • Every deliverable is a typed artifact with acceptance rules; every claim resolves to an evidence hash.

Module 01.1 03 / 10

The Death of Workshop Theater: Moving to an Executable Service OS

  • Sticky-note walls photograph well and query poorly — insight evaporates between the workshop and the backlog.
  • The fix is structural: typed, file-backed artifacts where the artifact itself is the handoff.
  • 32 registered artifact kinds (31 produced by the 74 methods) give every deliverable an enforceable schema.
  • Acceptance-unmet work is retained as draft with explicit failures — drift becomes visible instead of silent.
Raw insight→ Evidence store (SHA-256)→ Typed artifact + acceptance→ Queryable system of record
"The artifact is the handoff; everything else is commentary."
Module 01.2 04 / 10

Service-Dominant Logic & Value Co-Creation in the Age of AI

  • Vargo & Lusch (2004): value is never embedded in outputs — it is co-created in use (value-in-use).
  • Databases, forms, and scripts are operand resources: inert until acted upon.
  • Agents are operant resources: they act — mining evidence, drafting maps, checking their own drafts against rules.
  • The 74 registered methods are codified operant resources: structured value creation, invocable on demand.
Operand resources (data, forms)→ Operant agents (10 roles)⟷ Customer in context→ Co-created value-in-use
"Value is not what you deliver in a sprint; value is what the beneficiary experiences in context."
Module 01.3 05 / 10

The 10-Role Eve-Style Agent Topology: Role Contracts & Boundaries

  • A single generalist prompt pollutes its own context; specialized roles preserve investigative depth.
  • 10 role prompts ship with the runtime: researcher, librarian, synthesizer, mapper, ideator, prototyper, measurer, presenter, critic, orchestrator.
  • Every method contract pins an owner and a reviewGate — e.g. journey-from-transcripts: owner mapper, gate critic.
  • Handoffs are typed artifacts on disk, never conversational memory.
researcher→ synthesizer→ mapper (owner)→ critic (reviewGate)
Gate passArtifact accepted → status final, handoff proceeds
Gate vetoDefect rows with mandatory fixes → loop back to owner
"Specialized boundaries preserve depth and make severe adversarial critique possible."
Module 01.4 06 / 10

The Autonomy Spectrum & The Sovereign Human Decision Gate

  • High autonomy: ingestion, normalization, cross-referencing — mechanical, verifiable work.
  • Shared autonomy: agents draft, humans reshape — clustering, framing, blueprint assembly.
  • Sovereign human authority: sd_phase_advance fails closed without an approval receipt (receiptId, approvedBy, decision, rationale).
  • The spectrum is enforced by the runtime, not by convention or prompt etiquette.

Module 01.5 07 / 10

The Service Profit Chain: Linking Frontline Tooling to Customer Loyalty

  • Heskett et al. (1994): internal service quality drives employee satisfaction, which drives customer loyalty and profit.
  • Frontline teams armed with agent co-pilots escape screen tyranny and cognitive burnout.
  • The method service-profit-chain-mapping produces a service-profit-chain artifact linking internal tooling to financial outcomes.
  • Frontline tooling quality is a leading indicator of NPS and lifetime value, not an IT concern.
Internal agent tooling→ Frontline ease & retention→ First-contact resolution→ Loyalty → growth → profit
"Frontline tooling quality is the mathematical prerequisite for customer satisfaction."
