Paradigm Shift 01 / 06

The Death of Workshop Theater

  • Sticky-note workshops produce static artifacts that decay immediately upon project close.
  • Handoffs between research, mapping, and engineering suffer catastrophic information loss.
  • The Agentic Service Design OS replaces slides with executable, machine-checked filesystem models.
Workshop Theater (Static Slides / PDF) ➔ Agentic Service OS (Executable / Evidence-Gated)
"If a design artifact cannot be queried by an agent or audited by a CI gate, it is decorative art, not architecture."
Foundational Theory 02 / 06

Service-Dominant Logic & Co-Creation

  • Stephen Vargo & Robert Lusch (2004, 2014): Value is never embedded in outputs; value is co-created in use (*value-in-use*).
  • Operand resources (static databases, forms) require action to produce an effect.
  • AI agents act as active **operant resources**, initiating value co-creation with human customers and frontline staff.
Operant Agent Swarm ⟷ Customer in Context ➔ Co-Created Value
"Value is not what you deliver in a sprint; value is what the beneficiary experiences in context."
Agent Architecture 03 / 06

The 10-Role Eve-Style Topology

  • Single general-purpose LLM prompts fail on complex service design due to context pollution.
  • 10 specialized roles: Researcher, Librarian, Synthesizer, Mapper, Ideator, Prototyper, Measurer, Presenter, Critic, Orchestrator.
  • The core invariant: Handoffs are typed filesystem artifacts, never conversational memory.
Researcher (01) ➔ Synthesizer (05) ➔ Mapper (12) ➔ Critic Gate (38)
"Specialized boundaries preserve investigative depth and enable severe adversarial critique."
Human Governance 04 / 06

The Autonomy Spectrum & Decision Gates

  • High Autonomy: Transcript mining, quote normalization, graph traversal, telemetry cross-checks.
  • Shared Autonomy: How-Might-We generation, journey clustering, draft blueprint assembly.
  • Sovereign Human Authority: Phase gates, ethical risk sign-offs, trade-offs, and go/no-go pilot decisions.
Autonomous Discovery ➔ Sovereign Human Gate (Kind 37 Decision) ➔ Autonomous Execution
"Agents propose and verify with receipts; human leaders decide with accountability."
Enterprise Economics 05 / 06

The Service Profit Chain in AI Systems

  • Heskett et al. (1994): Internal service quality is the root driver of customer loyalty and enterprise profit.
  • Equipping frontline staff with AI co-pilots eliminates cognitive burnout and screen tyranny.
  • Frontline empowerment directly drives first-contact resolution, NPS, and lifetime value.
Internal Agent Tooling ➔ Frontline Staff Ease ➔ Customer Loyalty & Profit
"Frontline tooling quality is the mathematical prerequisite for customer satisfaction."
Enterprise Proof 06 / 06

Case Study: Calder Community Bank

  • Challenge: 6-week manual discovery cycle across 45 branches for SME account opening.
  • Agentic Sprint: 6 Researcher agents ingested 180 interview hours and 1,420 friction rows in 4 hours.
  • Outcome: Critic caught a 9.4-day KYC batch delay before pilot, enabling same-day onboarding architecture.
180h Interviews ➔ 1,420 Evidence Hashes ➔ Verified 5-Lane Blueprint
"100% receipt-backed confidence in 1/10th the time of traditional consulting."
