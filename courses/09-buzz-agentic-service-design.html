Course 09: BUZZ for Agentic Service Design

Run a receipted multi-agent engagement on a private team relay — ten role runtimes, fail-closed evidence handshakes, human phase gates

A complete, receipted BUZZ session: create the private engagement channel, install the ASD persona pack, dispatch scoped mentions to role runtimes, survive the fail-closed evidence handshake (mapper blocks, researcher audits and refuses to fabricate), verify sha256 store handoffs, close Discover through the Critic and a human approval receipt, and publish a hash-verified Canvas artifact index. Companion to Articles 37 and 39.

The Collaboration Layer 01 / 06

BUZZ: a private relay for your SD team

  • Identities are keypairs — role runtimes sign events as first-class members, not as your puppet.
  • One private channel per engagement: mentions route on pubkeys, Canvas carries the artifact index.
  • Every message and canvas set is a receipted event you can cite later.
""
The Roster 02 / 06

Ten role runtimes, two coding-agent advisors

  • The ASD persona pack installs through the desktop UI with a pack-local MCP config.
  • Mention by pubkey for automation; name-only mentions are presentation.
  • Only mentioned roles act — silent roles are an honest outcome, not a gap.
""
Live Session 03 / 06

Kickoff: sd/northfield-followup-care

  • The brief bounds scope, states signals as signals, and names human authority at every gate.
  • The orchestrator replies runtime-authored: signed by her key, referencing artifacts by path.
""
Fail-Closed 04 / 06

The evidence handshake

  • Mapper blocks rather than inventing pain points; researcher audits access and refuses to fabricate.
  • Owner posts store path + evidence ids; researcher reproduces every sha256 id from the store.
  • One round trip buys an engagement where every artifact names its hashed evidence.
""
Close Discover 05 / 06

Store artifacts and the human receipt

  • AEIOU synthesis final via Critic pass; factcheck exits 0 only when everything is final and resolvable.
  • The phase gate demands a full approval receipt — named human, timestamp, rationale.
""
Boundaries 06 / 06

Canvas is navigation, not proof

  • Render → set → get → verify: the content hash is the round-trip proof.
  • BUZZ proves collaboration; the ASD store proves state; ACP stays record-only.
""
