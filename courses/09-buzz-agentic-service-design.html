Course 09: BUZZ for Agentic Service Design

The platform stack, a complete receipted multi-agent engagement, and two live case studies — private team relay, ten role runtimes, fail-closed evidence handshakes, human phase gates

The platform stack and a complete, receipted BUZZ engagement in one course: the BUZZ relay and its proof boundaries, the ASD stack behind it (ten role agents, the pack-local MCP server, record-only ACP, the host integrations), then the live Aster Grove session end to end — scoped mentions, the fail-closed evidence handshake (the ideator refuses to fabricate), the deliberate veto and same-id remediation, the human approval receipt, and a hash-verified Canvas index. Closes with the Article 38 case study: the ASD Studio in the DeepSeek Harness and two live journeys. Companion to Articles 37, 38, and 39.

The Collaboration Layer 01 / 14

BUZZ: a private relay for your SD team

  • Identities are keypairs — role runtimes sign events as first-class members, not as your puppet.
  • One private channel per engagement: mentions route on pubkeys, Canvas carries the artifact index.
  • Every message and canvas set is a receipted event you can cite later — event IDs are citation formats.
Relay tm.communities.buzz.xyz→ Channels sd/<project>→ Keypair members→ Canvas index
"A BUZZ message can be a real collaboration event and still not be an ASD evidence row."

Canvas · Module 09.7 09 / 14

Render → set → get → verify

  • Generated from the store, never hand-written; fail-closed on any dangling id.
  • Twelve contract sections, from charter and gate state to roster participation and integrity hash.
  • Five verify checks: content hash, sections, stale links, round trip, drift.
  • Regenerated and re-verified after every workspace change — hashes 512a51c6… then fae1e45f….
"The Canvas points; the store proves."
The Refusal · Module 09.5 10 / 14

“I will not reconstruct evidence”

  • Rowan (ideator) could see the Canvas and the registry — but not the authoritative workspace.
  • He said so explicitly, mutated nothing, and refused to fabricate.
  • Maya escalated a clean blocker and paused Define rather than papering over the gap.
  • The boundary held under pressure — the strongest evidence the session produced.
"An agent that declines to fabricate is the system working, not failing."
Case Study II · Module 09.8 11 / 14

The Studio in the DeepSeek Harness

  • A full workbench lived in a 320-pixel sidebar — registered correctly, never activated.
  • The fix: additive center tab through the host's conversation.view seam; sidebar stays the fallback.
  • Activation, not registration, is where integration seams bite — twice in one article.
"The Studio was never mis-registered — it was never asked to appear."
