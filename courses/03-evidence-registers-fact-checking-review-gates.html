Course 03: Evidence Registers, Fact-Checking & Review Gates

SHA-256 Content-Addressed Evidence, The Critic Agent & Machine-Checkable Rigor

The rigor engine that prevents hallucinated AI design work. Master the core rule: 'Handoffs are typed artifacts, never chat'. Learn how content-addressed SHA-256 evidence registers store verbatim quotes, telemetry logs, and enterprise documents. Configure the adversarial Critic agent, manage review-veto-logs, and run deterministic sd_factcheck CI gates.
