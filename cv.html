Profile

Applied AI across the full lifecycle of enterprise problem-solving: research, synthesis, service mapping, blueprinting, software, prototypes, measurement.

Seven years across enterprise research, product design and service design — the last four building AI into the core of the practice, not the edges. At KFC South Pacific I set the service-design and research standard for an 800+ restaurant network: I frame the problems, run the evidence-gated practice and the agent tooling that scales it, and carry the work through executives, franchise governance forums and frontline teams to piloted, measured outcomes. I'm looking for a Head of or Principal Service Design role where an AI-first practice has to be built, led and proven — employee and customer experience, HR and P&C transformation, service design and UX.
