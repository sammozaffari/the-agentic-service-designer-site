Profile

Applied AI across the full lifecycle of enterprise problem-solving: research, synthesis, service mapping, blueprinting, software, prototypes, measurement.

Five-plus years across enterprise research, product design and service design; the last three and a half deliberately integrating AI into all of it: software development, research, prototyping and UI. The taste comes from a design background; the working method is agents: Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Gemini and a ten-role harness of my own design, with an adversarial critic on every gate. I work horizontally across the full lifecycle of enterprise problem-solving: research and synthesis, service mapping and blueprinting, building software and working prototypes, measurement and rollout. Hireable for HR and P&C AI transformation, engineering experience transformation, service design and UX. A team of one, plus as many agents as the job needs.
