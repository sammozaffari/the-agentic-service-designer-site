Sam Mozaffari

Service designer — AI-first & agentic practice. I run the full service design lifecycle through an agent team: research, synthesis, journey mapping, service blueprinting, co-design, prototyping, pilots, measurement and governance. Sydney, Australia · remote-friendly.

Profile

Service designer with a repeatable, documented, AI-native practice: 39 long-form published case studies across healthcare, insurance, banking, energy, logistics, public sector, industrial, mobility, education and more — every one executed with agent teams (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, GitHub, DeepSeek), every artifact evidence-cited and gated by an adversarial critic. I design where agents stop and humans step in: the autonomy spectrum from suggest to confirm to act, with trust, safety and explainability designed in.

Capabilities

  • Service design craft: end-to-end lifecycle, service blueprinting (including agent/LLM lanes), journey and experience mapping, problem framing, co-design workshops and facilitation, prototyping and piloting, systems thinking, futures thinking.
  • Agentic AI: architecting agentic service systems, agent orchestration, human-in-the-loop design, prompt frameworks, structured content models and schemas, MCP integration, evaluation frameworks and QA for agent output, AI prototyping, AI enablement and capability building.
  • Research & synthesis: research operations at scale (200-interview programs), mixed methods, evidence registers, insight formulation, personas and segmentation from evidence.
  • Delivery: agile delivery, stakeholder engagement to executive level, wave-gated rollouts, change packs, measurement rigs and KPI trees.
