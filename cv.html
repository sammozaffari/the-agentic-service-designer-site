Profile

Service designer who practices the discipline through AI agents and publishes the whole working. 36 long-form case studies across healthcare, insurance, banking, energy, logistics, public sector, industrial, mobility, education and more, every one executed with agent teams (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, GitHub, DeepSeek) and every artifact evidence-cited. I design where agents stop and humans step in: the autonomy spectrum from suggest to confirm to act, with trust, safety and explainability designed in.

I'm hired three ways: to deliver the work as a corporate engagement, to train teams to run the practice themselves, and to implement the toolbox inside a team's environment so they work agentically.

What I'm hired to do

  • Corporate engagements. End-to-end service design run agentically: research operations at scale, synthesis, journey mapping, blueprinting, prototyping, pilots, measurement and governance, delivered in days rather than months with evidence on every claim.
  • Training and enablement. Courses and workshops that take a design team through the toolbox: the ten-role harness, methods as code, evidence registers and review gates, so the team carries the practice into their own environment.
  • Agentic implementation. Setting the practice up where you work: packs for Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex and the DeepSeek Harness, an MCP server, a standalone CLI, templates and schemas, wired into your toolchain with CI.
